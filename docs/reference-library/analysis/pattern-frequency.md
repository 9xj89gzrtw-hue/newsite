# Pattern Frequency Analysis

> Common patterns observed across 22 competitor catering websites

## Hero Section Patterns

| Pattern | Frequency | Sites Using |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Full-width background image/video | 90% | Almost all |
| Centered headline + subheadline | 85% | WP, Gamma, JDK, etc. |
| Dual CTA buttons | 60% | WP, Gamma, Elegant Affairs |
| Single prominent CTA | 30% | GG Catering, Salt Block |
| Video background | 15% | Cut & Taste LV |

## Navigation Patterns

| Pattern | Frequency | Notes |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Sticky header on scroll | 80% | Industry standard |
| CTA button in header | 70% | "Start Planning", "Contact" |
| Dropdown mega-menu | 40% | JDK Group, Sopranos |
| Hamburger mobile menu | 100% | All responsive sites |
| Phone number in header | 50% | US-focused sites |

## Content Section Patterns

### Most Common Sections (in order)
1. **Services/What We Offer** - 100% of sites
2. **About/Our Story** - 95% of sites
3. **Gallery/Portfolio** - 85% of sites
4. **Testimonials/Reviews** - 70% of sites
5. **Events/Weddings focus** - 65% of sites
6. **Team/Meet the Chefs** - 50% of sites
7. **Venues/Locations** - 45% of sites
8. **Careers/Hiring** - 30% of sites
9. **Blog/News** - 25% of sites

## Color Palette Trends

### Premium/Luxury Segment (WP, Gamma, Elegant Affairs)
- Dark backgrounds (charcoal, navy)
- Gold/champagne accents
- Serif headlines
- Generous white space

### Modern/Fresh Segment (GG, Creative Edge, By Word of Mouth)
- White/light backgrounds
- Green or blue accent colors
- Sans-serif typography
- Friendly, approachable feel

### Bold/Energetic Segment (Sopranos, Cut & Taste)
- High contrast combinations
- Red/orange accent colors
- Large photography
- Action-oriented CTAs

## Typography Patterns

### Headline Fonts
- **Serif fonts**: 55% (Playfair Display, similar)
- **Sans-serif bold**: 35% (Montserrat, similar)
- **Custom/logotype**: 10%

### Body Fonts
- **Clean sans-serif**: 90% (Open Sans, Lato, Inter, similar)
- **Serif for body**: 10% (more traditional brands)

## CTA Button Patterns

### Text Copy Analysis
- "Contact Us" - most common (generic)
- "Start Planning" - action-oriented
- "Inquire / Enquiry" - premium feel
- "Book Your Event" - commitment-focused
- "Get a Quote" - price-focused

### Button Styling
- Solid fill with contrast: 70%
- Outline/hollow style: 20%
- Underlined text link: 10% (secondary CTAs)

## Mobile Patterns

### Mobile-Specific Features
- Sticky "Call Now" button: 40%
- Simplified navigation: 100%
- Tap-to-call phone numbers: 60%
- Swipeable galleries: 75%
- Collapsible sections: 50%
