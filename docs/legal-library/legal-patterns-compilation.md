## Privacy Policy Patterns

### Standard Sections for Catering Business Privacy Policy:

Based on analysis of Wolfgang Puck Catering, Relish Caterers, Cut & Taste, and Queen of Hearts Catering:

#### 1. Introduction/Who We Are
```markdown
At [Company Name], we collect and manage user data according to the following 
Privacy Policy. By using [website URL] (the "Website"), you agree to the terms 
of this Privacy Policy.
```

#### 2. Data Collection
**Common data points collected by catering websites:**
- Name and email address (for quotes/inquiries)
- Phone number
- Event details (date, guest count, venue)
- IP address and browser information
- Cookies and usage analytics

**Template language:**
```markdown
### Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:
- **Personal Information**: Name, email address, phone number, company name
- **Event Information**: Event type, date, expected guest count, venue preferences
- **Communication Data**: Messages sent through our contact forms

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our website:
- IP address, browser type, and device information
- Pages visited and time spent on our site
- Referring website address
```

#### 3. How We Use Data
**Common use cases for caterers:**
- Responding to event inquiries
- Sending quotes and proposals
- Marketing communications (with consent)
- Improving website functionality
- Analytics and reporting

**Template language:**
```markdown
### How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Process and respond to your event inquiries and quote requests
- Send you information about our services (with your consent)
- Improve our website and customer experience
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect against fraud and unauthorized transactions
```

#### 4. Data Sharing
**Typical third parties for caterers:**
- Email marketing platforms (Mailchimp, Constant Contact)
- CRM systems
- Payment processors
- Website analytics (Google Analytics)
- Social media platforms

**Template language:**
```markdown
### Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:
- **Service Providers**: Companies that help us operate our business 
  (email hosting, payment processing, analytics)
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers**: In connection with a merger or acquisition

We use [OneTrust/Termly/other] to manage consents and process your data.
```

#### 5. Data Security
```markdown
### Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
- Secure servers for data storage
- Access controls limiting employee access to personal data
- Regular security assessments
```

#### 6. Your Rights / Opt-Out Options
**Key rights to include (especially for GDPR/CCPA):**

```markdown
### Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your personal data
- **Opt-Out**: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Restrict**: Limit how we use your data

**California Residents (CCPA):** You have the right to know what personal 
information we collect, request deletion, and opt-out of the sale of personal 
information.

To exercise these rights, contact us at: [privacy@company.com]
```

#### 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)
```markdown
### Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not 
knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that 
we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please 
contact us immediately.
```

#### 8. Contact Information
```markdown
### Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us:

**[Company Name]**  
**Email:** privacy@company.com  
**Address:** [Street Address, City, State ZIP]  
**Phone:** [(XXX) XXX-XXXX]
```

## Terms of Service Patterns

### Standard Clauses for Catering Websites:

Based on Wolfgang Puck Catering's comprehensive terms:

#### 1. Acceptance of Terms
```markdown
### Terms of Use

Please read these Terms and Conditions of Use carefully. By accessing and using 
this website, you accept and agree to be bound by these terms.

This Agreement applies to your use of our website and any related services 
provided by [Company Name] ("we," "us," or "our").
```

```markdown
### Acceptable Use

You agree to use our website only for lawful purposes. You are prohibited from:
- Using the site in any way that violates applicable laws
- Attempting to gain unauthorized access to our systems
- Interfering with proper working of the website
- Scraping or extracting data without permission
```

```markdown
intellectual property laws.

You may not reproduce, distribute, modify, or create derivative works from our 
content without our prior written permission.
```

```markdown
### Disclaimer of Warranties; Limitation of Liability

Our website and content are provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis. 
We make no warranties, express or implied, regarding the operation of the site 
or the information contained herein.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, [Company Name] shall not be liable for:
- Any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages
- Loss of profits, data, or business opportunities
- Any damages arising from your use or inability to use the site
```

#### 5. Indemnification
```markdown
### Indemnification

You agree to indemnify and hold harmless [Company Name] and its officers, 
directors, employees, and agents from any claims, losses, or expenses arising 
from your use of the website or violation of these terms.
```

#### 6. Governing Law
```markdown
### Governing Law

These terms shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of 
[State/Country], without regard to conflict of law provisions.

Any disputes shall be resolved in the courts of [County, State].
```

#### 7. Changes to Terms
```markdown

We reserve the right to modify these terms at any time. Changes will be effective 
when posted on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of the 
site after changes constitutes acceptance of the new terms.
```

## Cookie Consent Best Practices

### Current State in Catering Industry:

**Finding:** Cookie consent banners were NOT detected in static HTML extraction from most sites. This suggests either:
1. Many US-based catering sites don't implement prominent cookie banners
2. Cookie banners are loaded dynamically via JavaScript
3. Some sites may not fully comply with cookie regulations

### Recommended Cookie Consent Implementation:

#### Banner Text Templates:

**Option A: Simple (US-focused)**
```html
<div class="cookie-banner">
  <p>We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze our traffic. 
  By clicking "Accept All", you consent to our use of cookies.</p>
  <button onclick="acceptCookies()">Accept All</button>
  <button onclick="declineCookies()">Decline</button>
  <a href="/privacy-policy/">Learn More</a>
</div>
```

**Option B: GDPR-Compliant (EU-focused)**
```html
<div class="cookie-banner">
  <h3>Cookie Settings</h3>
  <p>We use cookies and similar technologies to provide certain features, 
  enhance user experience, and analyze website traffic. You can choose to 
  accept all cookies or manage your preferences below.</p>
  
  <div class="cookie-categories">
    <label><input type="checkbox" checked disabled> Necessary</label>
    <label><input type="checkbox" checked> Functional</label>
    <label><input type="checkbox" checked> Analytics</label>
    <label><input type="checkbox"> Marketing</label>
  </div>
  
  <div class="cookie-buttons">
    <button id="accept-all">Accept All</button>
    <button id="save-preferences">Save Preferences</button>
    <button id="reject-all">Reject All</button>
  </div>
</div>
```

**Option C: CCPA-Compliant (California-focused)**
```html
<div class="ccpa-notice">
  <p>Your Privacy Choices (<a href="/privacy/#ccpa">Learn More</a>)</p>
  <button onclick="toggleOptOut()">
    <span aria-label="AdChoices">▲</span> Do Not Sell My Personal Information
  </button>
</div>
```

### Button Text Variations Found/Accepted:

| Action | Common Text Options |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Accept** | "Accept All", "I Agree", "Accept Cookies", "OK, Got It", "Continue to Site" |
| **Decline** | "Decline", "Reject All", "Only Necessary", "Continue Without Accepting" |
| **Settings** | "Settings", "Manage Preferences", "Customize", "Cookie Settings", "More Options" |

### Cookie Categories to Document:

```markdown
### Types of Cookies We Use

**Necessary Cookies (Always Active)**
- Session cookies for website functionality
- Authentication cookies for logged-in users
- Shopping cart/quote form persistence

**Functional Cookies**
- Remember user preferences (language, location)
- Enable chat/support features
- Form autofill assistance

**Analytics Cookies**
- Google Analytics or similar
- Traffic source tracking
- User behavior analysis

**Marketing Cookies**
- Social media integration cookies
- Retargeting/advertising pixels
- Conversion tracking
```

## GDPR/CCPA Compliance Notes

### GDPR Considerations (European Users):

**Key Requirements:**
1. **Lawful Basis for Processing** - Must document legal basis (consent, legitimate interest, contract)
2. **Data Subject Rights** - Must provide mechanism to exercise rights
3. **Data Protection Officer** - May need to appoint if processing at scale
4. **Breach Notification** - 72-hour notification requirement
5. **International Transfers** - Adequacy decisions or SCCs required

**For Catering Businesses:**
- Most catering companies serve local/regional markets
- If serving EU clients traveling to events, consider GDPR compliance
- Implement cookie consent with granular options
- Include GDPR-specific clauses if marketing to EU residents

### CCPA Considerations (California Residents):

**Key Requirements (as of 2024):**
1. **Notice at Collection** - Inform what data is collected before/during collection
2. **"Do Not Sell/Share" Option** - Provide opt-out mechanism
3. **Consumer Rights** - Access, deletion, correction, portability
4. **Non-Discrimination** - Cannot penalize for exercising rights
5. **Authorized Agent** - Allow representatives to act on behalf of consumers

**Implementation Checklist:**
- [ ] Add "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link in footer
- [ ] Create CCPA-specific privacy notice section
- [ ] Implement request submission form
- [ ] Train staff on handling consumer requests
- [ ] Verify third-party vendors' CCPA compliance

### Recommended Compliance Approach:

```markdown
## Privacy Notice Supplement

### For California Residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have 
the right to:

1. **Know** what personal information is being collected, sold, or disclosed
2. **Delete** personal information (with exceptions)
3. **Opt-Out** of the sale of personal information
4. **Non-Discrimination** for exercising privacy rights

**To exercise your rights:** Submit a request via [link/form] or call us at 
[phone number]. We will verify your identity before processing requests.

### For European Residents

We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You have the 
right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and 
object to processing of your personal data.

**Data Protection Representative:** [Name/Contact if applicable]
**Legal Basis for Processing:** [Consent/Legitimate Interest/Contract]
```

## Template Legal Text

### Complete Privacy Policy Template for Catering Business:

```markdown
# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]  
**Effective Date:** [Date]

## Introduction

Welcome to [Company Name] ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and 
are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how 
we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our 
website [URL] or use our catering services.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information You Provide
When you submit an inquiry, request a quote, or contact us, we may collect:
- **Contact Information:** Full name, email address, phone number
- **Company Information:** Company name, job title (for corporate events)
- **Event Details:** Event type, date, expected guest count, location preferences
- **Dietary Requirements:** Allergies, dietary restrictions, meal preferences
- **Payment Information:** Billing address, payment method (processed securely)

### Information Automatically Collected
When you visit our website, we automatically collect:
- Device information (browser type, operating system)
- IP address and geographic region
- Pages visited, time spent, and navigation paths
- Referring website or search terms

### Information from Third Parties
We may receive information from:
- Social media platforms (when you interact with our profiles)
- Marketing partners and referral sources
- Background check services (for employment)

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
1. Respond to event inquiries and prepare custom proposals
2. Process bookings and payments
3. Send relevant updates about our services (with consent)
4. Improve our website, menu offerings, and customer experience
5. Comply with legal obligations
6. Protect against fraud and unauthorized activities
7. Send transactional communications (order confirmations, reminders)

## Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not sell** your personal information. We may share data with:

**Service Providers:**
- Email marketing platforms ([Mailchimp/Constant Contact])
- Payment processors ([Stripe/PayPal/Square])
- Website hosting and analytics providers
- CRM and booking management systems

**Business Transfers:**
In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales

**Legal Requirements:**
When required by law, court order, or governmental authority

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures including:
- SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
- Secure payment processing (PCI DSS compliant)
- Access controls and authentication requirements
- Regular security reviews and updates

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:
- Fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

You may request deletion of your data at any time (subject to legal requirements).

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:
- **Access** a copy of your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Delete** your personal data
- **Opt-out** of marketing communications
- **Port** your data to another service
- **Restrict** certain types of processing
- **Object** to legitimate interests processing

**To exercise these rights:** Contact us at [privacy@email.com] or 
[mailing address]. We will respond within 30 days.

### California Residents (CCPA)
[Include specific CCPA rights and opt-out instructions]

### European Residents (GDPR)
[Include GDPR-specific rights and DPO contact if applicable]

## Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect

## Changes to This Policy
```
