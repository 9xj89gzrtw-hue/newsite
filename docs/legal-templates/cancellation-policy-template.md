## 6. Force Majeure (Circumstances Beyond Control)

### 6.1 Defined Events

Neither party liable for failure to perform due to:

**Natural Disasters:**
- Earthquakes, floods, hurricanes, tornadoes
- Wildfires, lightning strikes
- Extreme weather events (as declared by government)

**Government Actions:**
- Pandemics, epidemics, public health emergencies
- Government-mandated closures or gathering limits
- States of emergency, curfews, travel restrictions
- War, terrorism, civil unrest

**Infrastructure Failures:**
- Utility failures (power, water) affecting venue
- Transportation system failures
- Internet/communication outages (for virtual events)

**Personal Emergencies (Case-by-case):**
- Serious illness/injury to client or immediate family member (documentation required)
- Death in immediate family

### 6.2 Force Majeure Procedure

1. **Notification:** Affected party notifies other party within [X] hours of event/circumstance
2. **Documentation:** Written documentation required (government order, medical note, etc.)
3. **Resolution Options:**
   - **Reschedule** to new date with all payments credited (no penalties)
   - **Convert to alternative service** (delivery instead of full-service, etc.)
   - **Partial refund** less documented costs already incurred
   - **Full credit** toward future event within [X] months

### 6.3 COVID-19 / Infectious Disease Specific Provisions

If government mandates prevent your event:
- All payments convertible to future date credit (expires [X] months from original date)
- Option for meal kit delivery or drop-off catering instead
- If event cancelled more than [X] days out: Partial refund option available
- Deposits for COVID-related cancellations: Fully creditable (special exception)

## 7. Our Right to Cancel

### 7.1 Circumstances Where We May Cancel

We reserve the right to cancel only under these extreme circumstances:

- **Safety concerns** at venue (structural issues, safety hazards discovered)
- **Non-payment** per contract terms
- **Client misconduct** including abuse of staff, illegal activities planned
- **Force majeure** affecting our operations (kitchen damage, staff emergency)

### 7.2 If We Must Cancel

- **Full refund** of all payments made
- **Assistance** finding alternative caterer if possible
- **Priority rebooking** when we're able to resume services
