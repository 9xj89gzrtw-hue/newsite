## What Are Cookies?

**Cookies** are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, improve your browsing experience, and provide information to website owners.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can manage them.

---

## 1. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

### 1.1 Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `session_id` | Maintains your session while browsing | Session |
| `csrf_token` | Security protection against attacks | Session |
| `cookie_conent` | Stores your cookie preferences | 1 year |
| `acceptance_status` | Records if you've accepted cookies | 1 year |

**Cannot be disabled** - These are required for basic website functionality.

### 1.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance functionality and personalize your experience.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `language_pref` | Remembers your language choice | 1 year |
| `quote_data` | Saves your catering quote progress | 7 days |
| `event_date` | Stores event date during planning | Session |
| `menu_selections` | Remembers menu items you viewed | 30 days |
| `form_autofill` | Pre-fills forms with previous entries | 90 days |

### 1.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Provider |
|-------------|---------|----------|----------|
| `_ga` | Distinguishes users for analytics | 2 years | Google Analytics |
| `_ga_*` | Keeps session state | 2 years | Google Analytics |
| `_gid` | Distinguishes users | 24 hours | Google Analytics |
| `_gat` | Throttles request rate | 1 minute | Google Analytics |

**What we measure:**
- Pages visited and time on site
- Traffic sources (how you found us)
- Device types (mobile vs desktop)
- User paths through quote/request process
- Event types most frequently researched
- Geographic location of visitors (country/city level only)

**What we do NOT track:**
- Personally identifiable information via analytics
- Exact addresses or contact details
- Sensitive personal data

### 1.4 Marketing Cookies

These cookies deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign effectiveness.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Provider |
|-------------|---------|----------|----------|
| `_fbp` | Facebook ads tracking | 3 months | Meta/Facebook |
| `_fbc` | Facebook ad attribution | 3 months | Meta/Facebook |
| `fr` | Ad relevance | 3 months | Meta/Facebook |
| `_gcl_au` | Google Ads conversion linking | 3 months | Google |

**How marketing cookies work:**
- Show you our ads after visiting our site (retargeting)
- Measure effectiveness of advertising campaigns
- Limit how often you see the same ad
- Help us understand which ads lead to bookings

## 3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

### 3.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner offering:

- **Accept All Cookies** - Enables all cookie categories
- **Accept Essential Only** - Only necessary cookies
- **Customize Settings** - Choose which categories to accept
- **Decline All** - Reject non-essential cookies

Your preference is saved and respected on future visits.

### 3.2 Changing Preferences Later

You can update your cookie preferences at any time:

1. **Cookie Settings Link** - Found in footer of every page
2. **Browser Settings** - See Section 4 below
3. **Clear and Revisit** - Clear cookies and make new choices

### 3.3 Withdrawing Consent

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it:
- Click "Cookie Settings" in footer
- Uncheck categories you no longer consent to
- Save preferences
- Changes take effect immediately

## 4. Browser-Based Cookie Management

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

### 4.1 Browser-Specific Instructions

| Browser | Access Cookie Settings |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Chrome** | Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data |
| **Firefox** | Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data |
| **Safari** | Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data |
| **Edge** | Settings → Privacy → Cookies and site permissions |
| **Mobile Safari** | Settings → Safari → Prevent Cross-Site Tracking |

### 4.2 Browser Options Include:

- **Blocking all cookies** - May affect website functionality
- **Blocking third-party cookies** - Recommended balance
- **Deleting cookies on browser close** - Fresh start each session
- **Allowing/whitelisting specific sites** - Keep settings for trusted sites
- **Browsing in private/incognito mode** - No cookies saved after session ends

### ⚠️ Important Notice About Blocking Cookies

Disabling cookies may impact your ability to:
- Save catering quote progress
- Use online booking forms
- View embedded content (maps, videos)
- Receive personalized recommendations
- Complete contact forms properly

## 5. Cookie Lifecycle

### 5.1 Session Cookies

- Exist only while browser is open
- Deleted when you close browser
- Used for temporary data (shopping cart, form state)

### 5.2 Persistent Cookies

- Remain on device after browser closes
- Have set expiration dates
- Used for preferences, login status, analytics

### 5.3 First-Party vs Third-Party

| Type | Domain | Example Use |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **First-party** | Our domain (company.com) | Site preferences, login |
| **Third-party** | Different domain (google.com) | Analytics, advertising |

We respect Do Not Track (DNT) signals where legally required:

## 7. International Considerations

### 7.1 European Union (GDPR)

For EU visitors:
- Explicit consent required before non-essential cookies
- Must be opt-in (not opt-out)
- Easy withdrawal of consent
- Clear explanation of each cookie's purpose

### 7.2 California (CCPA/CPRA)

For California residents:
- Right to know what data is collected via cookies
- Right to opt-out of sale of data from cookies
- "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link provided
- Some cookie-based tracking considered "sale" under CCPA

### 7.3 Other Regions

- **UK:** Post-Brexit GDPR equivalent applies
- **Canada (PIPEDA):** Consent required for tracking cookies
- **Brazil (LGPD):** Similar requirements to GDPR
- **Various US States:** Evolving privacy laws; we aim for compliance across all
