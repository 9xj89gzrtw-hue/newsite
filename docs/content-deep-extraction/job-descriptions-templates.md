## Common Job Categories in Catering

### 1. Culinary Positions (Kitchen/Production)

#### Executive Chef
**Typical Companies:** Ridgewells, Wolfgang Puck, Concorde, Soprano's, JDK Group, Salt Block

**Template:**
```
Job Title: Executive Chef
Department: Culinary / Kitchen Operations
Reports To: General Manager / Director of Operations
Employment Type: Full-Time, Exempt
Salary Range: $70,000 - $100,000+ (varies by market/company size)

ABOUT THE ROLE:
We are seeking an experienced and creative Executive Chef to lead our 
culinary team and drive menu innovation. The Executive Chef is responsible 
for all aspects of kitchen operations, ensuring exceptional quality while 
maintaining food safety standards and cost controls.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
• Lead, mentor, and develop a culinary team of [X] staff members
• Design and execute menus for events ranging from intimate dinners to 
  galas for [X] guests
• Oversee food production across multiple concurrent events
• Manage food cost margins (typically 28-35% target)
• Ensure compliance with all health department regulations and HACCP protocols
• Collaborate with sales team on client presentations and tastings
• Source and maintain relationships with purveyors and suppliers
• Develop seasonal menus reflecting current trends and local availability
• Maintain inventory systems and minimize waste
• Train staff on proper techniques, safety, and dietary accommodations

REQUIREMENTS:
• Culinary degree (ACF certification preferred) or equivalent experience
• 7+ years progressive culinary experience
• 3+ years in a leadership/sous chef or higher role
• High-volume catering or hotel banquet experience required
• ServSafe Manager certification (or ability to obtain)
• Knowledge of dietary restrictions and allergen management
• Strong P&L management skills
• Physical ability to stand for extended periods and lift up to 50 lbs
• Flexibility to work evenings, weekends, and holidays as needed

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:
• Experience with multiple cuisine types
• Background in upscale dining or hotels
• Menu engineering and costing software proficiency
• Local market knowledge and supplier relationships

BENEFITS:
• Competitive salary + performance bonus potential
• Comprehensive health (medical/dental/vision) insurance
• 401(k) with company match
• Paid time off and holidays
• Professional development opportunities
• Employee meal program
• Uniform program
```

#### Banquet Captain / Server
**Template:**
```
Job Title: Banquet Captain / Server
Department: Service
Reports To: Event Manager / Director of Service
Employment Type: Part-Time / On-Call / Seasonal
Compensation: $18-$28/hour + gratuities (varies significantly)

BANQUET CAPTAIN RESPONSIBILITIES:
• Lead service team during events ([X]-[Y] servers typical)
• Conduct pre-event briefings with service staff
• Ensure proper setup according to event order
• Coordinate timing with kitchen and event manager
• Address guest needs and resolve issues promptly
• Maintain service standards throughout event
• Oversee breakdown and cleanup

SERVER RESPONSIBILITIES:
• Set up events per diagram/specifications
• Serve food and beverages professionally
• Respond to guest requests courteously
• Maintain knowledge of menu items including allergens
• Clear plates and maintain table presentation
• Assist with event breakdown

REQUIREMENTS:
• Previous fine dining, banquet, or catering service experience
• Professional appearance and demeanor
• Physical ability to carry trays, stand for long periods
• Availability for evenings, weekends, and some weekdays
• Reliable transportation
• Positive, team-oriented attitude

SCHEDULE:
Events typically include:
• Evenings (setup begins 2-6 hours before event time)
• Weekends (peak wedding/corporate event days)
• Some daytime events (luncheons, brunches)
• Holiday periods (especially November-December)
```

## Hiring Trends in Catering Industry

### Current Demand Areas (2024-2025)

1. **Executive Chefs** - Always in demand, especially those with catering experience
2. **Experienced Event Managers** - Shortage of qualified coordinators
3. **Sales Professionals** - Those who understand events, not just product sales
4. **Dietary Specialists** - Chefs/managers with deep allergy knowledge
5. **Sustainability Coordinators** - Emerging role at larger companies

### Skills Increasingly Valued

- **Technology proficiency** - Event software, POS systems, CRM
- **Dietary/allergen expertise** - Critical differentiator
- **Multilingual abilities** - Especially Spanish in many markets
- **Social media savvy** - Creating content, managing brand presence
- **Flexibility/adaptability** - Post-pandemic industry changes

### Compensation Trends

- **Upward pressure on wages** - Labor shortage affecting all levels
- **Sign-on bonuses** - More common for hard-to-fill roles
- **Performance bonuses** - Expanding beyond just sales roles
- **Equity/profit sharing** - At senior levels and some private companies
- **Transparent pay bands** - Growing trend toward salary transparency

## Application & Interview Process

### Typical Stages:

1. **Application** - Online application, resume, sometimes cover letter
2. **Phone Screen** - HR or hiring manager, 15-30 minutes
3. **In-Person/Virtual Interview** - With direct manager, potentially team
4. **Practical Component** (for culinary/service):
   - **Chefs:** Stage/trial shift, cooking demonstration
   - **Service:** Mock event scenario, role-play
   - **Sales:** Mock sales call, proposal exercise
5. **Reference Check** - Professional references verified
6. **Background Check** - Criminal, sometimes credit (for financial roles)
7. **Offer** - Verbal then written offer

### What Employers Look For:

**For All Roles:**
- Reliability and punctuality track record
- Positive attitude and teamwork
- Genuine interest in food/hospitality
- Flexibility with schedule
- Professional presentation

**For Leadership Roles:**
- Problem-solving examples
- Conflict resolution experience
- Mentoring/development of others
- Strategic thinking ability
- Cultural fit with company values
