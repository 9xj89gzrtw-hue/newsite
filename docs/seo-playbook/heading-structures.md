## Industry Best Practices Identified

### H1 Patterns
Based on analysis of top catering sites:

1. **Brand + Value Proposition** (Most Common)
   - Format: `[Brand Name] Catering | [Value Proposition]`
   - Example: `Wolfgang Puck Catering | The Standard for Culinary Excellence`

2. **Service + Location** (Local SEO Focus)
   - Format: `[Service Type] Catering [Location Area]`
   - Example: `Sopranos catering is the premier catering service for southeast Michigan`

3. **Descriptive Statement**
   - Format: `[Action/Adjective] + [Service] + [Location/Scope]`
   - Example: `Top Quality Full Service Off-Premise Catering With Elegant Affairs`

### H2 Section Categories (Most Common)
1. **Services Offered** - Wedding catering, corporate events, social events
2. **About/Story** - Company history, team introduction
3. **Menu/Food** - Cuisine types, sample menus
4. **Testimonials/Social Proof** - Client reviews, featured events
5. **Contact/CTA** - Get a quote, contact information
6. **Gallery/Portfolio** - Event photos, food photography

## Recommended Heading Template for Catering Homepage

```
H1: [Brand Name] | Premium [Location/Area] Catering Services

H2: Award-Winning Catering for Every Occasion
  H3: Wedding Catering
  H3: Corporate Events & Business Catering  
  H3: Social Events & Private Parties
  
H2: Why Choose [Brand Name]
  H3: Farm-Fresh Ingredients
  H3: Custom Menu Creation
  H3: Full-Service Experience
  
H2: Our Menus

```

## Key Findings

| Metric | Best Practice | % Sites Compliant |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| Single H1 per page | ✅ Exactly 1 H1 | ~60% |
| H2 count (homepage) | 5-10 sections | ~75% |
| H3 subsections | Use under relevant H2s | ~55% |
| Keyword in H1 | Primary keyword present | ~85% |
| Location in H1/H2 | Geographic modifier | ~70% |
