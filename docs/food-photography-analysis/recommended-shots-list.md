## Russian Market Specific Recommendations

### Cultural Adaptations for Photography

**Visual Preferences:**

1. **ABUNDANCE SIGNALS GENEROSITY**
   - Full plates, not minimal plating
   - Overflowing displays
   - Height and volume in buffets
   - Generous portion sizes shown

2. **WARMTH AND HOSPITALITY**
   - Warm color tones preferred
   - Candlelight appreciation
   - Cozy atmosphere over stark modern
   - Human elements included

3. **QUALITY THROUGH DETAILS**
   - Premium materials visible (crystal, silver)
   - Fresh flowers prominently featured
   - Impeccable table linens
   - White glove service cues

4. **TRADITION MEETS MODERNITY**
   - Respect for classical dishes
   - Modern presentation techniques
   - International cuisine competence
   - Local specialties honored

### Key Occasion Photography Priorities

**#1 NEW YEAR CORPORATE (Новый год)**
- MOST IMPORTANT for B2B revenue
- Shoot timing: November-December
- Must convey: Celebration, luxury, success
- Colors: Gold, red, deep green, white
- Elements: Champagne, caviar, elaborate spreads

**#2 SUMMER WEDDINGS (Летние свадьбы)**
- Peak season: June-August
- Must convey: Romance, beauty, elegance
- Outdoor garden settings prized
- Floral emphasis critical
- Light, airy, dreamy aesthetic

**#3 MILESTONE CELEBRATIONS (Юбилеи)**
- Round numbers (30, 40, 50, etc.)
- Family-oriented imagery
- Warm, personal feeling
- Customization examples
- Multi-generational appeal

### Avoid for Russian Market

❌ Minimalist/scant portions (signals cheapness)  
❌ Stark cold aesthetics (feels unwelcoming)  
❌ Too casual/sloppy (unprofessional perception)  
❌ Excessive modern/experimental (may alienate)  
❌ Dark/moody food photos (unappetizing)

## Technical Quick Reference

### Camera Settings Cheat Sheet

| Shot Type | Aperture | ISO | Shutter | Focus |
|-----------|----------|-----|---------|-------|
| Food macro | f/4-f/5.6 | 100 | 1/125 | Single pt |
| Food hero | f/2.8-f/4 | 200 | 1/160 | Single pt |
| Buffet wide | f/5.6-f/8 | 400 | 1/100 | Zone |
| Event wide | f/4-f/5.6 | 1600 | 1/200 | AI Servo |
| Detail shot | f/2.8 | 400 | 1/200 | Single pt |
| Portrait/team | f/2-f/2.8 | 400 | 1/250 | Eye AF |

```
[DATE]_[CATEGORY]_[SUBJECT]_[VERSION]
```
