## Executive Summary

Event photography serves as social proof for catering companies, demonstrating their ability to execute at scale across various venue types and occasion styles. This analysis covers best practices for capturing and presenting event photography on catering websites.

## 2. Shot Types for Event Photography

### 2.1 Establishing Shots (Wide)

**Purpose:** Show scale, venue, overall atmosphere

| Shot Type | Description | Camera Position |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Venue Overview | Full room capture | Back of room, elevated |
| Guest Arrival | Entrance perspective | Near entry point |
| Grand Reveal | Room before guests | Center, eye-level |
| Exterior Context | Building/venue exterior | Distance, ground level |

**Technical Specs:**
- Lens: 16-35mm wide angle
- Aperture: f/8-f/11 for depth
- ISO: 400-800 typical
- Include architectural features

### 2.2 Medium Shots

**Purpose:** Show interactions, service in action

| Shot Type | Description | Framing |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Service Action | Staff serving guests | Waist up |
| Guest Interaction | Guests with food/drink | Groups of 2-4 |
| Station Detail | Food station with context | 3/4 view |
| Table Setting | Complete place setting | 45° angle |

**Technical Specs:**
- Lens: 35-85mm standard
- Aperture: f/4-f/5.6
- ISO: 800-1600 for low light
- Capture ambient atmosphere

### 2.3 Detail Shots (Close)

**Purpose:** Showcase quality, craftsmanship, attention to detail

| Shot Type | Description | Focus Point |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| Plating Detail | Single dish artistry | Center plate |
| Garnish Close-up | Finishing touch | Macro distance |
| Glassware | Drink presentation | Rim or liquid |
| Linen/Fold | Napkin artistry | Texture detail |
| Place Card | Personalization element | Text readable |

**Technical Specs:**
- Lens: 85mm macro or 100mm
- Aperture: f/2.8-f/4
- Shallow DOF for separation
- Clean background essential

## 3. Lighting Considerations for Events

### 3.1 Working with Available Light

**Advantages:**
- Authentic atmosphere preservation
- Less intrusive to event
- Natural color temperature
- Guest comfort maintained

**Challenges:**
- Variable quality
- Mixed color sources
- Low light situations
- Harsh shadows possible

**Solutions:**
- Shoot during golden hours when possible
- Use fast lenses (f/1.4-f/2.8)
- Embrace ambient mood
- Supplement subtly with reflectors

### 3.2 Event Lighting Integration

**Uplighting (Walls):**
- Creates dramatic atmosphere
- Can cast color tints
- Work WITH not against it
- Note color for white balance

**Pin Spotting (Tables):**
- Creates focused illumination
- Good for table detail shots
- Can be harsh if direct
- Use as accent, not key light

**String Lights/Fairy Lights:**
- Popular for weddings
- Beautiful bokeh opportunity
- Warm color temperature
- Shoot wide open for effect

**Candlelight:**
- Romantic ambiance
- Very warm (2700K+)
- Low light challenge
- Use for mood, supplement for detail

### 3.3 Flash Usage Guidelines

**When to Use Flash:**
- Fill shadows in harsh daylight
- Freeze action moments
- Balance mixed lighting
- Create consistent coverage

**When to Avoid Flash:**
- During speeches/ceremonies
- When it disrupts atmosphere
- With sensitive lighting designs
- During emotional moments

**Techniques:**
- Bounce flash when possible
- Use diffusers (MagMod, etc.)
- Off-camera for directionality
- Low power for subtle fill

## 4. Composition for Event Photography

### 4.1 Telling the Story

**Narrative Sequence:**
1. **Before:** Setup, preparation, anticipation
2. **Arrival:** Guests entering, first impressions
3. **Service:** Food being enjoyed, staff in action
4. **Atmosphere:** Dancing, laughing, celebrating
5. **Details:** The small things that matter
6. **After:** Successful event evidence

### 4.2 Layered Compositions

**Foreground-Middle-Background:**
- Foreground: Blur element for depth (guest back, decor)
- Middle: Main subject (food station, speaker, couple)
- Background: Venue context, other guests, architecture

**Leading Lines:**
- Tables create natural lines
- Aisle leads to altar/stage
- Buffet line draws eye
- Architectural features guide viewer

### 4.3 Rule of Space

**For Moving Subjects:**
- Leave space in direction of movement
- Guest walking right → space on right
- Server carrying tray → space ahead
- Dancer spinning → space in rotation direction

**For Looking Subjects:**
- Leave space where person is looking
- Speaker addressing audience → audience space
- Guest viewing food → food space

## 5. Capturing Catering-Specific Elements

### 5.1 Food Stations in Event Context

**Essential Shots:**

1. **The "Hero" Wide Shot**
   - Entire station in frame
   - Signage visible
   - Staff positioned well
   - Queue showing popularity (if appropriate)
   - Venue context included

2. **The Detail Sequence**
   - Overview of offerings
   - Close hero dish
   - Being served action
   - Guest receiving plate
   - First bite/enjoyment

3. **The Process Documentation**
   - Chef preparing/cooking
   - Plating technique
   - Garnish application
   - Presentation completion

### 5.2 Service in Action

**What to Capture:**
- Professional, polished staff appearance
- Graceful serving technique
- Guest interaction warmth
- Efficiency without rushing
- Attention to detail
- Problem-solving moments (subtle)

**What to Avoid:**
- Empty chafing dishes
- Messy service areas
- Stressed expressions
- Clumsy handling
- Unattended stations

### 5.3 Guest Experience Documentation

**Appropriate Captures:**
- Smiling faces with food
- Conversational groups
- Toasting moments
- Applause/reaction shots
- Candid laughter
- Children enjoying (with permission)

**Privacy Considerations:**
- Focus on permission-granted guests
- Blurred backgrounds when needed
- Back/side profiles often safer
- Group shots distribute focus
- Always have event photo policy

## 6. Post-Processing for Event Photos

### 6.1 Consistent Style Development

**Create Event Presets:**
- Base correction for venue lighting
- Color tone matching brand
- Consistent exposure approach
- Signature look development

**Typical Adjustments:**

| Parameter | Wedding | Corporate | Social |
|-----------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Exposure | +0.3 | 0 | +0.2 |
| Contrast | +10 | +15 | +5 |
| Highlights | -30 | -20 | -25 |
| Shadows | +25 | +15 | +20 |
| Temperature | Warm +8 | Neutral | Warm +5 |
| Tint | Magenta +3 | 0 | Magenta +2 |
| Clarity | +10 | +15 | +8 |
| Vignette | Light | Moderate | Light |

### 6.2 Batch Processing Workflow

1. **Select best images** from each category
2. **Apply base preset** to all selected
3. **Individual tweaks** for special photos
4. **Crop for consistency** across gallery
5. **Export with proper settings**
6. **Rename with convention**
7. **Backup to multiple locations**

## 8. Russian Market Event Photography Priorities

### 8.1 Key Event Types for Russian Market

**Priority 1: New Year Corporate (Новогодний корпоратив)**
- Most important B2B revenue period
- Expected: Luxury, abundance, celebration
- Colors: Gold, red, green, white
- Must show: Premium service, impressive spread

**Priority 2: Summer Weddings (Летние свадьбы)**
- Peak wedding season June-August
- Expected: Elegance, romance, beauty
- Style: Sophisticated, dreamy
- Must show: Beautiful venues, exquisite food

**Priority 3: Milestone Birthdays (Юбилеи)**
- Major life celebrations
- Expected: Warmth, family, joy
- Style: Personal, festive
- Must show: Customization options

**Priority 4: Business Events (Деловые мероприятия)**
- Growing corporate market
- Expected: Professionalism, efficiency
- Style: Clean, modern
- Must show: Capability, reliability

### 8.2 Cultural Visual Preferences

**Abundance = Generosity:**
- Full tables, not minimal plating
- Multiple course visibility
- Height variation in displays
- Overflowing bowls/baskets

**Quality Signals:**
- Fresh flowers prominently featured
- Quality glassware/crystal
- White glove service cues
- Impeccable table linens
- Silver/chafing dish presence

**Atmosphere Elements:**
- Warm lighting preference
- Candlelight appreciation
- Live music context (when applicable)
- Guest engagement visible
- Celebration energy captured

## 9. Equipment Recommendations

### 9.1 Camera Body

**Professional Level:**
- Sony A7R IV/A7R V (high res, great AF)
- Canon R5/R6 (reliable, excellent color)
- Nikon Z7/Z8 (dynamic range leader)

**Prosumer Alternative:**
- Sony A7III/A7IV (great value)
- Canon R/RP (user friendly)
- Nikon Z6/Z6II (low light king)

### 9.2 Lens Kit for Event Coverage

**Essential Lenses:**

| Focal Length | Purpose | Max Aperture |
|--------------|---------|--------------|
| 16-35mm | Wide venue shots | f/2.8 or f/4 |
| 24-70mm | Workhorse zoom | f/2.8 |
| 70-200mm | Telephoto reach | f/2.8 |
| 35mm or 50mm | Low light prime | f/1.4 or f/1.8 |
| 85mm or 90mm | Portraits/details | f/1.8 or macro |

### 9.3 Support & Accessories

**Stabilization:**
- Monopod for mobility
- Small tripod for details
- Gimbal for video (if offering)

**Lighting:**
- Speedlight(s) with modifiers
- Flashpoint/Godox system
- MagMod modifiers
- Reflector kit (5-in-1)

**Other Essentials:**
- Extra batteries (minimum 4)
- High-capacity memory cards
- Card reader for backup
- Rain cover for outdoor events
- Lens cleaning kit
