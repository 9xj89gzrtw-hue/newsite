## 1. Photography Style Categories

### 1.1 Professional Studio Style
**Characteristics:**
- Controlled lighting environment
- Perfectly styled compositions
- Clean, distraction-free backgrounds
- High-end commercial aesthetic

**Best For:**
- Menu item showcases
- Website hero images
- Print materials
- Social media content

**Leading Examples:**
- Wolfgang Puck Catering (Hollywood glamour aesthetic)
- Ridgewells (Clean, corporate-professional)
- Relish Caterers (Modern, vibrant)

**Characteristics:**
- Natural or ambient lighting
- Slightly imperfect, authentic feel
- Environmental context included
- Warm, inviting atmosphere

**Best For:**
- About page storytelling
- Blog content
- Social media engagement
- Brand personality

**Leading Examples:**
- My Radish (Farm-to-table aesthetic)
- Queen of Hearts Catering (Home-style warmth)
- Tall Guy and a Grill (Casual BBQ vibe)

### 1.3 Event Documentary Style
**Characteristics:**
- Captured during actual events
- Candid guest interactions
- Authentic atmosphere documentation
- Varied lighting conditions

**Best For:**
- Portfolio galleries
- Case studies
- Client testimonials
- Social proof

## 2. Technical Specifications Analysis

### 2.1 Camera Settings Trends

| Shot Type | Aperture | Shutter Speed | ISO | Focus |
|-----------|----------|---------------|-----|-------|
| Food Close-up | f/2.8 - f/4 | 1/125 - 1/250 | 100-400 | Single point, center |
| Buffet Wide | f/5.6 - f/8 | 1/60 - 1/125 | 400-800 | Zone focus |
| Action/Chef | f/4 - f/5.6 | 1/250+ | 800-1600 | Continuous AF |
| Table Details | f/4 - f/5.6 | 1/60 - 1/125 | 200-600 | Selective focus |

### 2.2 Image Resolution Standards

**Minimum Acceptable:**
- Web display: 1200 x 800 px (72 ppi)
- Retina-ready: 2400 x 1600 px (144 ppi)
- Print quality: 3000 x 2000 px (300 ppi)

**Hero Image Recommendations:**
- Homepage sliders: 1920 x 1080 px minimum
- Gallery thumbnails: 600 x 400 px
- Social media: 1080 x 1080 px (square) or 1200 x 628 px (landscape)

### 2.3 File Formats

| Format | Use Case | Quality Setting |
|--------|----------|-----------------|
| JPEG | General web use | 85-92% |
| WebP | Modern browsers | 80-85% |
| PNG | Graphics with text | Lossless |
| AVIF | Next-gen optimization | 70-80% |

## 3. Composition Patterns

### 3.1 Rule of Thirds in Food Photography

**Application:**
- Place main dish at intersection points
- Lead eyes with utensils or garnishes
- Balance negative space effectively
- Create visual flow through composition

### 3.2 Layering Technique

**Elements from bottom to top:**
1. Surface/background texture
2. Base plate or vessel
3. Main food item
4. Sauce drizzle or plating
5. Garnish and finishing touches
6. Props (linens, glassware, cutlery)

### 3.3 Angle Variations

| Angle | Best For | Effect |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Overhead (0°) | Flat-lays, pizzas, patterned dishes | Graphic, modern |
| 45° | Most food photography | Natural viewing angle |
| 30° | Stacked dishes, burgers | Appetizing height |
| Eye-level (90°) | Beverages, tall items | Dramatic presence |

## 5. Lighting Techniques

### 5.1 Natural Light Setup

**Window Light (Preferred):**
- Position table near large window
- Use sheer curtain as diffuser
- Add white reflector opposite window
- Shoot during morning or afternoon golden hours

### 5.2 Artificial Light Setup

**Single Softbox:**
- 45-degree angle from subject
- Large modifier (24"+) for soft shadows
- Fill card on opposite side
- Background light optional

**Two-Light Professional:**
- Key light: 45° camera left, softbox
- Fill light: camera right, reflected or diffused
- Rim/hair light: behind subject, for separation

### 5.3 Common Lighting Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Mixed color temperatures** - Keep all lights same Kelvin
2. **Harsh overhead lighting** - Creates unappetizing shadows
3. **Direct flash** - Too harsh, flat appearance
4. **Insufficient fill** - Deep shadows lose detail
5. **Colored ambient spill** - Affects food color accuracy

## 6. Props and Styling

### 6.1 Essential Prop Categories

**Surface Props:**
- Marble slabs (white, gray, black)
- Wood boards (light oak, walnut, reclaimed)
- Concrete tiles
- Textured linens
- Slate plates

**Vessel Props:**
- Ceramic plates (various colors/sizes)
- Cast iron skillets
- Copper pots
- Glass bowls
- Wooden serving boards

**Supporting Props:**
- Vintage cutlery
- Linen napkins
- Fresh herb sprigs
- Condiment bottles
- Wine glasses
- Serving utensils

### 6.2 Styling Best Practices

1. **Prep multiple versions** of each dish
2. **Use food-safe tweezers** for precise placement
3. **Have oil spray ready** for sheen
4. **Work quickly** with temperature-sensitive foods
5. **Keep paper towels handy** for cleanup
6. **Build composition in layers**

## 7. Post-Processing Guidelines

### 7.1 Standard Adjustments

| Adjustment | Typical Range | Purpose |
|------------|---------------|---------|
| Exposure | +0.3 to +0.7 EV | Brighten slightly |
| Contrast | +5 to +15 | Add depth |
| Highlights | -20 to -40 | Recover detail |
| Shadows | +10 to +30 | Open dark areas |
| Clarity | +5 to +15 | Add texture |
| Vibrance | +10 to +20 | Enhance colors |
| Saturation | -5 to +5 | Fine-tune intensity |
| Sharpness | +20 to +40 (output) | Crisp finish |

### 7.2 Color Grading Styles

**Warm & Inviting:**
- Temperature: +5 to +10
- Tint: +2 to +5 (magenta)
- HSL: Boost oranges and yellows
- Tone warm highlights

**Fresh & Clean:**
- Temperature: 0 to +3
- Lift blacks slightly
- Boost greens and teals
- Clean shadows

**Moody & Dramatic:**
- Temperature: -3 to -5
- Crush blacks slightly
- Desaturate except accent color
- High contrast

## 9. Hero Image Criteria

### What Makes a "Hero Quality" Food Photo:

✅ **Technical Excellence:**
- Sharp focus on key area
- Proper exposure (no blown highlights)
- Appropriate depth of field
- No motion blur (unless intentional)
- Clean noise profile

✅ **Compositional Strength:**
- Clear focal point
- Balanced visual weight
- Leading lines guide eye
- Appropriate negative space
- Works at multiple sizes

✅ **Emotional Impact:**
- Triggers appetite response
- Conveys quality/craftsmanship
- Matches brand aesthetic
- Tells a story
- Memorable/distinctive

✅ **Practical Utility:**
- Versatile cropping potential
- Text overlay space available
- Consistent with other assets
- Optimized file size
- Current/timely styling
