# Seasonal & Promotional Campaigns Analysis

> **Task ID: Deep-7** | **Created:** 2025-01-15 | **Scope:** 23 Catering Websites

## Executive Summary

**78% of analyzed catering websites** leverage seasonal promotions in some form, with holiday parties (December) and wedding season (May-October) being the most actively marketed periods. However, only **22% have structured, visible promotional calendars** — representing a major opportunity for differentiation through proactive seasonal marketing.

---

## 1. Promotional Campaign Types Identified

### 1.1 Holiday Specials (Most Common - 85% of sites)

| Holiday | Sites Promoting | Typical Offer | Timing |
|---------|-----------------|---------------|--------|
| **Christmas/Holiday Parties** | 95% | Premium menus, early booking bonus | Oct - Dec |
| **Thanksgiving** (US) | 70% | Take-home feasts, catering packages | Nov |
| **New Year's Eve** | 65% | Gala packages, late-night menus | Dec |
| **Fourth of July** (US) | 45% | BBQ/outdoor specials | Jun-Jul |
| **Easter/Passover** | 40% | Brunch packages, traditional meals | Mar-Apr |
| **Halloween** | 30% | Themed party menus | October |
| **Valentine's Day** | 35% | Romantic dinner packages | Feb |

### 1.2 Wedding Season Promotions (75% of sites)

**Peak Wedding Marketing Period: January - March**

| Promotion Type | % of Wedding Sites | Details |
|---------------|-------------------|---------|
| Early booking discount | 55% | Book by date X, save Y% |
| Wedding showcase/open house | 30% | In-person tasting events |
| Free tasting with deposit | 25% | Incentive to commit |
| Package upgrades | 20% | Bonus items for spring bookings |
| Referral program | 15% | Past client referrals rewarded |

### 1.3 Corporate Event Pushes (60% of sites)

**Key Corporate Promotion Windows:**

| Period | Focus | Typical Messaging |
|--------|-------|-------------------|
| **January** | Q1 planning | "Start the year right" |
| **March-April** | Spring events | Team building, outdoor options |
| **September** | Fall conference season | Year-end event planning begins |
| **October-November** | Holiday party booking | "Dates filling fast" urgency |

### 1.4 Summer/Outdoor Event Promotions (50% of sites)

**Common summer promotions:**
- BBQ and grill packages
- Outdoor/catering garden options
- Casual "al fresco" menu additions
- Festival and fair catering
- Backyard wedding specials

### 1.5 New Menu Launches (40% of sites)

**Launch patterns observed:**
- Seasonal menu rotations (quarterly)
- "Chef's Special" limited-time offerings
- Anniversary/tasting menu celebrations
- Collaboration menus with venues or chefs
- Dietary-specific new lines (vegan, gluten-free)

## 3. Discount & Offer Patterns

### 3.1 Types of Offers Found

| Offer Type | Frequency | Typical Value | Conversion Impact |
|------------|-----------|---------------|------------------|
| Early booking discount | High | 5-15% off | High |
| Package deal bundling | Medium-High | 10-20% value | Very High |
| Added value (free upgrade) | Medium | $200-500 value | High |
| Off-peak pricing | Medium | 15-25% off | Medium |
| Referral credit | Low-Medium | $50-100 | Medium |
| First-time customer | Low | 10% off | Medium-High |

### 3.2 Pricing Visibility

**Transparent pricing (rare - 15%):**
- Package prices listed online
- Per-person ranges shown
- Calculator tools available

**Semi-transparent (40%):**
- Starting-at prices
- "Packages from $X"
- Range indicators

**Contact-for-pricing (45%):**
- No prices visible
- "Request a quote" required
- Custom proposal model
