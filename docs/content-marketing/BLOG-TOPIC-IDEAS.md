## Topic 1: Complete Guide to Wedding Catering in Saint Petersburg

**Category:** Wedding | **Funnel Stage:** Awareness
**Primary Keyword:** кейтеринг на свадьбу Санкт-Петербург (wedding catering Saint Petersburg)
**Word Count Target:** 2,500-3,500 words

### Outline

```
I. INTRODUCTION (150 words)
   - Hook: Your wedding feast sets the tone for the entire celebration
   - Stats: Average wedding has X guests, Y budget on catering
   - What this guide covers
   - [CTA: Jump to section of interest]

II. WHY CATERING MATTERS FOR WEDDINGS (200 words)
   - Food is #1 thing guests remember
   - Photos of food last forever
   - Dietary accommodations show care for guests
   - Service quality reflects on the couple

III. TYPES OF WEDDING CATERING FORMATS (400 words)
   A. Seated Dinner (Formal)
      - Traditional multi-course
      - Best for: Formal venues, older guests
      - Pros/cons
   
   B. Stations Style (Interactive)
      - Action stations, grazing tables
      - Best for: Modern couples, mingling preference
      - Pros/cons
   
   C. Cocktail Reception (Casual-Elegant)
      - Passed appetizers, small plates
      - Best for: Shorter receptions, late weddings
      - Pros/cons
   
   D. Family-Style (Warm & Intimate)
      - Platters shared at tables
      - Best for: Rustic/intimate vibes
      - Pros/cons

IV. PLANNING TIMELINE: WHEN TO BOOK (300 words)
   - 12+ months out: Research and shortlist
   - 9-12 months: Book caterer, schedule tasting
   - 6-9 months: Finalize menu concept
   - 3-6 months: Menu tasting, final selections
   - 1-3 months: Final headcount, dietary needs
   - 1 week before: Confirm all details

V. BUDGET CONSIDERATIONS (350 words)
   - Cost per person ranges in SPb market
   - What affects price (menu complexity, service level, rentals)
   - Where to splurge vs. save
   - Hidden costs to watch for
   - Payment schedules typical

VI. MENU PLANNING ESSENTIALS (400 words)
   - Seasonal considerations (what's best each season)
   - Balancing crowd-pleasers with special touches
   - Dietary accommodation planning
   - Drink package decisions
   - Late-night snack options
   - Cake/dessert coordination

VII. QUESTIONS TO ASK YOUR CATERER (300 words)
   - Top 10 questions with why each matters
   - Red flags to watch for
   - What answers should sound like

VIII. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID (250 words)
   - Underestimating quantities
   - Not accounting for dietary needs
   - Last-minute changes
   - Not tasting beforehand
   - Ignoring service flow

IX. SAINT PETERSBURG-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS (200 words)
   - Venue peculiarities in SPb
   - Seasonal weather impacts
   - Local specialty options
   - Working with historic venues

X. CONCLUSION + NEXT STEPS (150 words)
   - Recap key points
   - Encouragement to start planning
   - [CTA: Schedule consultation / View wedding menu]
   - [Related posts links]

RESOURCES:
├── Downloadable: Wedding Catering Checklist PDF
├── Link to: Wedding Gallery page
├── Link to: Real Weddings case studies
└── Link to: Contact/Tasting request form
```

## Topic 2: 10 Essential Questions to Ask Your Caterer Before Booking

**Category:** Wedding/Corporate | **Funnel Stage:** Consideration
**Primary Keyword:** вопросы кейтерингу (questions to ask caterer)
**Word Count Target:** 1,800-2,200 words

### Outline

```
I. INTRODUCTION (100 words)
   - Why asking right questions matters
   - This list saves time, money, stress

II. THE 10 QUESTIONS (main body - ~150 words each)

   1. "What's included in your base price?"
      - What to listen for
      - Red flag answers
      - Follow-up probes
   
   2. "Have you worked at our venue before?"
      - Why venue experience matters
      - What if they haven't?
   
   3. "How do you handle dietary restrictions?"
      - Allergen protocols
      - Cross-contamination prevention
   
   4. "What does a typical service timeline look like?"
      - Arrival, setup, breakdown
      - Overtime policies
   
   5. "Can we customize the menu?"
      - Flexibility levels
      - Change deadlines
      - Modification fees
   
   6. "Who will be our point person day-of?"
      - Communication structure
      - Backup plans
   
   7. "Do you provide rentals or do we source separately?"
      - Linens, china, glassware
      - Coordination benefits of one vendor
   
   8. "What's your backup plan for emergencies?"
      - Staff illness
      - Equipment failure
      - Weather contingencies
   
   9. "Can we speak with recent clients?"
      - Reference policies
      - What to ask references
   
   10. "What makes you different from other caterers?"
       - USP identification
       - Authenticity check

III. BONUS: 5 QUESTIONS THEY SHOULD ASK YOU (200 words)
    - Shows you're prepared
    - Helps them serve you better

IV. CONCLUSION (100 words)
    - Summary
    - [CTA: Ask us these questions - schedule a call]

RELATED:
├── Wedding Planning Timeline post
├── How to Compare Catering Quotes post
└── Red Flags When Hiring Caterers (future post)
```
