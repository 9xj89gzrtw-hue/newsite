RELATIONSHIPS FIRST

At The JDK Group Catering & Events, our team realizes that every event is a cherished moment to celebrate. We believe that no matter the size or function -- from a small corporate catered dinner to an over-the-top mitzvah -- it is crucial to build a meaningful relationship first.  Our people-focused philosophy helps us to understand your purpose in order to create a stress-free event designed around your needs.

WORKING FOR YOU

We have celebrated with thousands of people throughout our 25 year journey in the catering and event industry and our mission still remains the same. Our one goal is to serve you and your guests by producing a flawless event. This begins with execution in mind from the aid of our catering, combat-trained team of event storytellers. They will create a comprehensive proposal detailing the services necessary to produce your special event.

PASSION DRIVES US

The passionate and talented team members we employ have become our greatest asset. Our people take priority when it comes to investing back into the business. We hyperfocus on in-house training and industry education to propel our passion to produce events and serve our clients. Because of this, we could never truly put a price on the value of our people and it’s one of the many reasons why we will never compromise our service standards.

CREDIBILITY YOU DESERVE

For over 25 years, The JDK Group team continues to evolve our services to meet the catering and event needs of our clients and the communities of Harrisburg, Lancaster, and York. Since our early beginnings in hospitality, we have annually given back to the local community through service and event contributions, employed multiple industry-accredited and award-winning talent, received local and international accolades, and produced over 20,000 events.
