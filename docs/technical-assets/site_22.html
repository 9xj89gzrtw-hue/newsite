SUMMER

Inspired by a rainbow of farmers market fruits and vegetables, our summer menus highlight bright, jewel-toned produce, bringing you the freshest dishes of the year. Leave the cooking to us and let the summer barbeques begin.

View Summer Menu

SPRING

Our spring menus bring fresh-from-the-garden young springtime ingredients including leafy green vegetables, tender stalks and shiny pods to the table. Inspired by the first al fresco dining of the new year, our spring menus offer a refreshing change from the rustic and comfort offerings of winter.

View Spring Menu

FALL

As the seasons change and entertaining moves indoors, enjoy a fall-harvest feast with ingredients kissed by the last of the summer sun. Earthy flavors, brightly colored squashes and rustic meats bring the first of your favorite cold-weather favorites to the table.

View Fall Menu

WINTER

Let our winter menus warm your appetite with slow cooked dishes featuring the hearty ingredients of seasonal harvests. Seared roasts, our signature pot pie and classic cocktail offerings are finished with bright citrus and fresh herbs, bringing holiday cheer to every event.

WHO WE ARE

Wolfgang Puck has revolutionized the dining and events landscapes with a singular focus: to exceed expectations whenever our guests gather for a meal. Austrian-born Chef Wolfgang Puck founded Wolfgang Puck Catering in 1998, bringing best-in-class catering and dining services to diverse environments. Our team continues to set the standard for culinary excellence, bringing Wolfgang’s legendary combination of innovative cuisine and refined service to the worlds’ most renowned and demanding corporate, cultural and entertainment clients.
