Luxury Catering and Event Management

For four decades, By Word of Mouth has been a leading name in the events industry as both a luxury catering and event management specialist.  We are known for delivering exceptional detail, creativity and style, along with unmatched levels of knowledge and experience.

We are proud to be a certified B Corp.

We cater and co-ordinate events, weddings and celebrations of all kinds in private homes, offices and many of the UK’s most prestigious venues. Whatever your entertaining needs, our expert team would be delighted to chat over your requirements and help you create an unforgettable event.
