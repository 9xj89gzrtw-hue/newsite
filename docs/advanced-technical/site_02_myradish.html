Radish offers flexible, tailored, next-generation campus dining solutions that bring the best of the Bay Area's culinary culture into your workplace. We manage & operate full-service campus cafes; offer office lunch catering that is unlike anything else you’ve experienced; and serve as a one-stop shop for meetings, events, happy hours, snack & beverage programs and more.

Meeting services, happy hours and conferences are just the beginning. Cooking demonstrations by Top Chef Alumni, global finger foods by La Cocina entrepreneurs, and signature dishes from Michelin-starred chefs make events for employees, guests and clients into seriously unforgettable experiences.

Radish is the only employee dining program in the Bay that brings the signature dishes of celebrity chefs into your office, alongside recipes by local immigrant entrepreneurs. Our unique culinary collaboration model is the foundation of everything we do – and you won’t find anything like it anywhere else.

We love serving values-aligned clients. If you’re the same, you should know that Radish operates an SF Green Certified kitchen. We donate tens of thousands of meals annually to folks facing food insecurity in the Bay through our partnership with Replate. And we are a proud member of 1% For the Planet. We’ve pioneered a more ethical model for driving revenue to local restaurants. And every Radish meal is served in reusable or compostable vessels.
