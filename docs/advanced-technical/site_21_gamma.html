About us
Enquiry
Since 1986, GAMMACATERING has been creating unique moments of indulgence and holistic event experiences for guests with the highest expectations. Catering, space, design and guest care come together with us as one harmonious whole. We accompany you personally, always, with great sensitivity and an eye for every last detail.

SERVICES

GAMMACATERING is full-service catering. Concept, design and delivery come from a single source. The result: perfectly attuned experience worlds that stay in the memory.

Concept

We do far more than food and beverage. With an eye for the whole, GAMMACATERING develops event concepts that bring culinary vision, spatial design and dramaturgy together. In the concept phase, we define and align the culinary lead idea, service format, running order, guest experience and budget. Menus, beverage pairings and service flows are considered together from the very beginning, so that everything plays in harmony.

Design

GAMMADESIGN develops visual design concepts for events, spaces and staging. This covers not only table styling, floristry and event decoration, but also spatial concepts, scenography, materiality, furnishing, lighting mood and interior design. These services can be booked as part of a catering project or entirely independently – for example, for private or commercial interior design commissions.

Implementation

A seamless event requires precision and experience. This is exactly what GAMMACATERING stands for. We take care of the operational preparation as well as the on-site delivery of the event – including kitchen, service, partner coordination, and set-up and take-down. Our aim is that our clients can enjoy their event without a care in the world, safe in the knowledge that everything is working perfectly behind the scenes.
