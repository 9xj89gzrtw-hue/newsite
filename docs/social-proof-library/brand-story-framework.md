# Brand Story Framework Template

> **Purpose:** Ready-to-use framework for crafting a compelling catering brand story  
> **Based On:** Analysis of 23 top catering websites' brand narratives

## Section-by-Section Guide

### 1. THE HOOK (Your Tagline/Mission Statement)

**Purpose:** Capture attention in one line

**Examples from Analyzed Sites:**

| Company | Hook | Why It Works |
|---------|------|--------------|
| JDK Group | "Our mission is celebrating you" | Simple, client-centered |
| Wolfgang Puck | "Setting the standard for culinary excellence" | Authority positioning |
| Ridgewells | "95+ years of impeccable hospitality" | Longevity as differentiator |
| Tall Guy | "Only certified green caterer in Wisconsin" | Unique niche ownership |
| Queen of Hearts | "Easy planning, peace of mind" | Addresses pain points |

**Template Options:**

**Option A - Mission-Focused:**
```
"[Verb]ing [audience] through [differentiator]"
Examples:
- Celebrating life's moments through exceptional cuisine
- Elevating corporate events through culinary excellence
- Creating wedding memories through farm-fresh flavors
```

**Option B - Outcome-Focused:**
```
"[Adjective] [service] for [audience] who want [desire]"
Examples:
- Flawless catering for couples who want stress-free weddings
- Innovative cuisine for companies who want memorable events
- Sustainable catering for hosts who care about impact
```

**Option C - Heritage-Focused:**
```
"[Timeframe] of [quality] [service] in [location]"
Examples:
- Three decades of award-winning catering in Philadelphia
- Family-owned excellence since 1986
- Generations of culinary tradition, modern execution
```

### 2. THE ORIGIN (Your Founding Story)

**Purpose:** Establish authenticity and human connection

**Key Elements to Include:**

| Element | Questions to Answer |
|---------|---------------------|
| **When** | Year founded? What was happening then? |
| **Who** | Founder(s) name(s)? Background? |
| **Where** | Started where? Still there? |
| **Why** | Gap in market? Personal passion? Problem encountered? |

**Origin Story Patterns Found:**

**Pattern A: The Chef's Vision** (Wolfgang Puck style)
> *"Austrian-born Chef [Name] founded [Company] in [Year], bringing [unique approach] to [market]."*

**Pattern B: The Family Legacy** (Common approach)
> *"What started as [humble beginning] in [year] has grown into [current state], but our commitment to [core value] hasn't changed."*

**Pattern C: The Problem Solver** (JDK Group style)
> *"We noticed that [target audience] struggled with [problem]. We knew there had to be a better way - one that prioritized [value]."*

**Pattern D: The Passion Project** (Tall Guy style)
> *"We believed that [industry] could be more [adjective]. More [value]. So we built the company we wished existed."*

**Template - Fill in the Blanks:**

```
It started in [YEAR] when [FOUNDER NAME(S)] ...

[Choose one origin narrative:]

□ ... decided to turn a lifelong passion for [food/hospitality/events]
   into a business that could bring joy to others.

□ ... saw a gap in the [local/regional] catering market for 
   [specific type of service/food/style].

□ ... was catering [first event] for [who] when they realized 
   this was their calling.

□ ... brought together [number] years of experience in 
   [related field] to create something new.

The original vision was simple: [ONE SENTENCE DESCRIBING 
ORIGINAL GOAL].

[LOCATION detail]: We started out of [original location/kitchen/
venue] serving [original client type].
```

### 3. THE STRUGGLE (The Problem You Solved)

**Purpose:** Show you understand client pain points

**Common Catering Industry Struggles to Reference:**

| Client Pain Point | How Top Brands Address It |
|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Stressful planning | "Easy planning, peace of mind" (Queen of Hearts) |
| Generic/bland food | "Creative, innovative menus" (Multiple sites) |
| Unreliable service | "Flawless execution" promise (JDK, Cut & Taste) |
| Hidden costs | Transparent pricing messaging |
| Dietary limitations | Custom menu solutions |
| Impersonal experience | "Event storytellers" framing (JDK) |

**Template:**

```
We knew that [TARGET AUDIENCE] deserved better than:

✗ [Industry problem 1 - e.g., cookie-cutter menus]
✗ [Industry problem 2 - e.g., unreliable service]  
✗ [Industry problem 3 - e.g., hidden fees/stress]

We'd seen too many [events/clients] settle for [mediocre outcome].
There had to be a better way.
```

### 4. THE SOLUTION (Your Unique Approach)

**Purpose:** Explain what makes you different

**Differentiation Categories:**

| Category | Examples from Sites |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Food Philosophy** | Farm-to-fork (Tall Guy), From scratch (multiple), Chef-driven (SaltBlock) |
| **Service Style** | Event storytellers (JDK), White-glove (implied), Full-service (Elegant Affairs) |
| **Values Alignment** | Certified green (Tall Guy), Seed-oil-free (SaltBlock), Sustainable (GG Catering) |
| **Experience Level** | 95 years (Ridgewells), 20,000 events (JDK), Since 1986 (Gamma) |
| **Celebrity/Brand** | Wolfgang Puck name, Award-winning teams |

**Template:**

```
So we built [COMPANY NAME] on a foundation of [CORE VALUE]:

[Choose your pillars - typically 3:]

PILLAR 1: [FOOD APPROACH]
e.g., "Every dish is made from scratch using locally-sourced, 
seasonal ingredients."

PILLAR 2: [SERVICE APPROACH]  
e.g., "Our team doesn't just cater events - we tell your story 
through food and experience."

PILLAR 3: [VALUE/DIFFERENTIATOR]
e.g., "We're committed to [sustainability/personal service/
innovation] in everything we do."
```

### 5. THE MISSION (What Drives You Today)

**Purpose:** Connect emotionally with values-aligned clients

**Mission Statement Templates:**

**Format A - Action + Impact:**
```
"To [ACTION] [AUDIENCE] through [MEANS] so they can [OUTCOME]."

Examples:
- To celebrate clients through exceptional cuisine so they can 
  be fully present at their own events.
- To elevate corporate gatherings through innovative food experiences 
  so companies can build meaningful connections.
```

**Format B - Belief-Led:**
```
"We believe that [BELIEF STATEMENT]."

Examples:
- We believe that every event deserves food that creates memories.
- We believe that great catering should be stress-free, not stressful.
- We believe that how you feed people shows how much you care.
```

**Format C - Simple/Powerful:**
```
"[VERB] [NOUN]."

Examples:
- Celebrating you. (JDK Group)
- Food that connects. (hypothetical)
- Excellence, always. (hypothetical)
```

**Values Statement Template:**

```
OUR VALUES GUIDE EVERYTHING WE DO:

❤️ [VALUE 1] - [One sentence explanation]
   Example: Quality First - We never cut corners on ingredients 
   or service.

🤝 [VALUE 2] - [One sentence explanation]  
   Example: Relationships Over Transactions - Every client 
   becomes part of our family.

💡 [VALUE 3] - [One sentence explanation]
   Example: Creative Innovation - We're always evolving and 
   improving what we offer.

🌱 [VALUE 4] - [One sentence explanation]
   Example: Community Commitment - We invest in the place 
   we call home.
```

### 6. THE PROOF (Credibility & Social Proof)

**Purpose:** Back up claims with evidence

**Elements to Include:**

```yaml
PROOF POINTS:
  longevity: "[X]+ years serving [region/community]"
  volume: "[X],000+ events catered"
  recognition: "[Award names and years]"
  clients: "[Notable client names/types]"
  team: "[X] dedicated professionals, [certifications]"
  reviews: "[X]-star rating across [number] reviews"
  media: "[Publication/Media mentions]"
```

### 7. THE VISION (Where You're Headed)

**Purpose:** Show ambition and forward momentum

**Template Options:**

**Option A - Growth-Focused:**
```
"As we look to the future, we're excited to [EXPANSION PLAN]
while staying true to the values that got us here. Our goal is 
to become [ASPIRATION] for [TARGET AUDIENCE] in [REGION/NICHE]."
```

**Option B - Impact-Focused:**
```
"Our vision is simple: to make every event we touch a little 
more [ADJECTIVE: memorable/delicious/special]. We'll keep 
innovating, keep improving, and keep treating every client 
like family."
```

**Option C - Community-Focused:**
```
"We're proud to call [COMMUNITY] home, and we're committed to 
growing alongside it. In the coming years, you'll see us 
[SPECIFIC PLANS: expanding our team/adding services/opening 
new locations/deepening community involvement]."
```

## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Tone Options:

| Tone | Best For | Example Phrases |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| **Warm & Personal** | Weddings, social events | "We'd love to be part of your special day" |
| **Professional & Polished** | Corporate, galas | "Excellence in every detail" |
| **Passionate & Artistic** | Creative, high-end | "Culinary artistry for your table" |
| **Friendly & Approachable** | Local, casual | "Good food, good times, no stress" |
| **Heritage & Traditional** | Established brands | "A tradition of quality since [year]" |

### Do's and Don'ts:

**DO:**
- ✅ Use specific details (not vague claims)
- ✅ Include human elements (names, stories)
- ✅ Show vulnerability (struggles overcome)
- ✅ Be authentic to who you are
- ✅ Keep it scannable (paragraphs, headers)

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Use corporate jargon
- ❌ Make claims you can't back up
- ❌ Copy competitors' stories
- ❌ Write a novel (keep it focused)
- ❌ Hide your personality
