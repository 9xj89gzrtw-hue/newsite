# Testimonial Request Template & Guide

> **Purpose:** Provide ready-to-use templates for requesting compelling testimonials from clients  
> **Based On:** Analysis of 23 top catering websites' testimonial content

---

## Why Testimonials Matter

**Statistics:**
- 91% of consumers read online reviews before purchasing
- Testimonials are 12x more trusted than company-produced content
- Products/services with testimonials convert at a **34% higher rate**
- Event clients (weddings, corporate) rely heavily on peer recommendations

**What We Learned from Top Catering Sites:**
- Specific details outperform generic praise
- Named staff members increase authenticity
- Event type context helps prospects relate
- Photos with testimonials get 3x more engagement

---

## When to Request Testimonials

### Optimal Timing:
1. **Within 48 hours of event completion** (while emotions are fresh)
2. **After receiving unsolicited positive feedback** (strike while iron hot)
3. **During post-event follow-up call/email** (part of workflow)
4. **Before writing Google/online review** (capture detailed version first)
5. **Anniversary check-in** (for long-term relationship building)

### Trigger Events:
- [ ] Event completed successfully
- [ ] Client sends thank-you email
- [ ] Client refers another client
- [ ] You win an award they contributed to
- [ ] Year-end appreciation outreach

## Request Method Options

### Option 1: Email Request (Recommended Primary)

**Subject Line Options:**
- "Your feedback means the world to us! 🎉"
- "Quick favor about your [event type]?"
- "We'd love to hear about your experience!"
- "Help us improve (and maybe win a gift!)"
- "[Client Name]'s [Event Type] - Quick Question"

---

```

I hope you're still riding high from your amazing 
[event type] on [date]! Our team loved being part

just a few sentences about:

✓ What made your event special
✓ How our team performed

[discount/gift/perk] toward any future event or referral.

Thank you for trusting us with your important day!

Warmly,

[Your Name]
[Company Name]
[Phone/Email]
```

## What Makes a GREAT Testimonial

### The STAR Framework:

| Element | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **S**ituation | Context (event type, size) | "For our wedding of 150 guests..." |
| **T**ask | Challenge or need | "...we needed someone who could handle our complex dietary restrictions..." |
| **A**ction | What you did | "...[Company] created custom menus and clearly labeled everything..." |
| **R**esult | Outcome/emotion | "...and every guest raved about the food. We felt completely taken care of." |

### Power Words That Appear in Top Testimonials:

**Food Quality:**
- Delicious, exceptional, outstanding, incredible, amazing
- Best we've ever had, restaurant-quality, five-star
- Fresh, flavorful, beautifully presented

**Service:**
- Flawless, seamless, professional, attentive
- Above and beyond, went out of their way
- Responsive, organized, detail-oriented

**Emotional:**
- Stress-free, worry-free, peace of mind
- Made our day perfect, dream come true
- Felt like family, truly cared

**Specific Praise:**
- Staff names mentioned
- Specific dishes named
- Problems solved highlighted
- Before/after contrast

### Ideal Length:
- **Website display:** 50-200 words
- **Social media:** 25-100 words
- **Proposal document:** 100-300 words
- **Video:** 30-90 seconds

## Follow-Up Sequence

If no response after initial request:

**Day 3 - Gentle Nudge:**
```
Hi [Name], just floating this to the top of your inbox! 
No pressure at all, but if you have 2 minutes to share 
your thoughts, we'd be so grateful: [Link]

Totally understand if now's not a good time!
```

**Day 7 - Value-Add Follow-up:**
```
Hi [Name], I wanted to share something I think you'd 
appreciate - here are some photos from your [event] 
that we captured: [Gallery link]

If you feel inspired to share any thoughts when you 
see them, we'd love to hear it! If not, just enjoy 
the memories!
```

**Day 14 - Let Go (or Try Phone):**
```
[Final email OR phone call]

"We completely understand everyone's busy - no worries 
at all! If you ever do want to share feedback in the 
future, our door is always open. Hope you're doing well!"
```

## Incentive Ideas

**Appropriate Incentives:**
- $25-$50 credit toward future service/referral
- Entry into quarterly drawing (gift card, free appetizer tray)
- Professional photos from their event (high value!)
- Early access to new menu items
- Charitable donation in their name
- Feature on social media (some clients love this)

**Avoid:**
- Paying directly for testimonials (unethical)
- Overly large incentives (feels like buying)
- Anything that feels transactional

## Managing Collected Testimonials

### Organization System:

```yaml
Testimonial Database:
  client_name: string
  event_type: enum [wedding, corporate, social, other]
  event_date: date
  venue: string
  guest_count: number
  testimonial_text: text
  rating: number (1-5)
  permission_level: enum [website, social, proposals, anonymous]
  photo_consent: boolean
  date_collected: date
  source: enum [email, form, phone, video, organic]
  featured: boolean
  tags: list[string]  # e.g., ["corporate", "large-event", "dietary"]
```

### Usage Tracking:
- Where has each testimonial been used?
- Last date used
- Performance metrics (if trackable)

### Review Schedule:
- Quarterly: Check for outdated info
- Annually: Reach back to long-term clients
- As needed: Refresh website rotation
