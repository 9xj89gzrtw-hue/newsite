## Executive Summary

### Key Findings:

1. **Pricing Transparency**: Most catering websites do NOT show specific prices on their homepage
2. **"Contact for Quote" Dominant**: The primary approach is to encourage inquiry-based quoting
3. **Package Mentions Common**: Many sites mention "packages" without detailing prices
4. **Seasonal Promotions**: Some sites highlight seasonal specials or menus
5. **Per-Person Language**: When pricing is discussed, "per person/guest" is the standard unit

## Common Pricing Patterns Across Industry

### 1. Pricing Display Strategies

#### Strategy A: Fully Transparent (Rare)
- Shows per-person pricing clearly
- Lists package tiers with prices
- Includes sample menus with costs
- *Best for: High-volume, standardized offerings*

#### Strategy B: Semi-Transparent (Common)
- Shows "starting at" prices
- Provides price ranges ($X - $Y per person)
- Mentions minimum guest counts
- Requires contact for exact quote
- *Best for: Mid-to-high end caterers*

#### Strategy C: Inquiry-Based (Most Common)
- No prices shown
- "Contact for custom quote" messaging
- Focus on value and experience
- Qualifies leads before discussing budget
- *Best for: Premium/bespoke caterers*

### 2. Pricing Language Patterns

| Phrase Type | Examples | Psychological Effect |
|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Investment | "Your investment in..." | Elevates perceived value |
| Starting At | "Packages starting at $X" | Sets floor expectation |
| Custom Quote | "Tailored to your needs" | Personalization signal |
| Per Person | "$X per guest" | Clear unit economics |
| Packages | "Silver/Gold/Platinum" | Choice architecture |

### 3. Package Tier Naming Conventions

Common tier names found across sites:
- **Basic/Essential/Classic** → Entry level
- **Premium/Deluxe/Elevated** → Mid-range  
- **Elite/Luxury/Bespoke/Premium+** → High-end
- **All-Inclusive/White Glove** → Full service

### 4. Fee Disclosure Best Practices

Based on industry patterns, transparent disclosure includes:
- Base per-person cost
- Service charge percentage (typically 18-22%)
- Gratuity suggestions
- Minimum guest requirements
- Deposit amounts (usually 25-50%)
- Overtime rates
- Travel/delivery fees for off-site events

### 5. Seasonal Pricing Signals

Patterns observed:
- **Holiday Premiums**: +10-25% for December events
- **Off-Season Discounts**: Winter months (Jan-Mar)
- **Peak Day Surcharges**: Saturdays in May-October
- **Early Booking Incentives**: Book 6+ months ahead

## Recommended Pricing Page Structure

```
1. HERO SECTION
   - Value proposition headline
   - "Starting at $X" or "Custom Quotes Available"
   - CTA: Get Your Quote / View Packages

2. PACKAGE TIERS (if showing prices)
   - 3-4 clear tiers
   - What's included in each
   - Per-person pricing
   - "Most Popular" badge

3. WHAT'S INCLUDED
   - Menu customization options
   - Service staff details
   - Equipment provided
   - Setup/cleanup coverage

4. ADDITIONAL SERVICES
   - Bar service upgrades
   - Rental coordination
   - Floral/decor partnerships
   - Day-of coordination

5. INVESTMENT DETAILS
   - Payment schedule
   - Deposit requirements
   - Cancellation policy
   - What's NOT included

6. SOCIAL PROOF
   - Testimonials mentioning value
   - Gallery of events at various price points

7. CTA SECTION
   - Contact form
   - Phone number
   - "Download Sample Menu" lead magnet
```
