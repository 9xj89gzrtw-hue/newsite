# Menu Presentation Patterns Analysis

**Generated:** 2026-01-20  
**Sources Analyzed:** 15 catering websites

---

## Executive Summary

### How Catering Sites Present Their Menus:

1. **Format Variety**: Mix of PDF downloads, web pages, and image galleries
2. **Organization Methods**: By event type, season, course, or package tier
3. **Dietary Labeling**: Most sites mention vegetarian/vegan/gluten-free options
4. **Customization**: "Build your own" or "customized to your needs" common
5. **Beverage Pairings**: Often mentioned but rarely detailed online
6. **Tastings**: Usually require inquiry/booking, not self-service

## Customization Communication

### Copy Examples for Menu Pages:

#### Emphasizing Flexibility:
- "Every menu is a starting point. Let's make it yours."
- "Don't see exactly what you want? We love customization requests."
- "Our chefs thrive on creating dishes tailored to your vision."
- "Mix and match from any category. No restrictions."

#### Process Explanation:
```
CUSTOMIZATION MADE SIMPLE

STEP 1: BROWSE
Explore our sample menus for inspiration

STEP 2: SELECT  
Choose items that speak to you (or mix categories!)

STEP 3: CUSTOMIZE
Tell us about dietary needs, preferences, must-haves

STEP 4: FINALIZE
We'll create your custom proposal within 48 hours
```
