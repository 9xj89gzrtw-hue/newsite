## Email Capture Best Practices Observed

### Placement Locations:
1. **Footer**: Standard newsletter signup
2. **Hero Section**: Primary CTA alongside main offer
3. **Blog Posts**: Inline content upgrades
4. **Exit Intent**: Popup before leaving
5. **Menu Pages**: "Download full menu" form
6. **About Page**: "Learn more about us" signup
7. **Contact Page**: Additional newsletter option

### Conversion Optimization Tips:
- **Reduce fields**: Name + Email only for newsletters
- **Value proposition clear**: State benefit above fold
- **Privacy assurance**: Include unsubscribe note
- **Social proof**: Show subscriber count or testimonials
- **Instant gratification**: Deliver lead magnet immediately
- **Mobile optimized**: Large touch targets, simple layout

### Privacy/Legal Language Examples:
- "We respect your privacy. Unsubscribe anytime."
- "No spam, ever. Just delicious updates."
- "By signing up, you agree to our Privacy Policy."
- "We'll never share your information."
