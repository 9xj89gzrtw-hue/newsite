### Russian Wedding Customs & Cultural Notes

> **IMPORTANT CULTURAL CONTEXT FOR RUSSIAN-SPEAKING CLIENTS:**
>
> **The Role of Tamada (Тамада)**  
> Russian weddings traditionally feature a tamada – a master of ceremonies who leads toasts, organizes games, and keeps the celebration flowing. Unlike Western MCs who mainly announce events, tamadas are entertainers who shape the entire reception experience. Catering teams should coordinate closely with the tamada on timing.
>
> **Extended Celebration Times**  
> Russian wedding banquets often last 8-12+ hours (sometimes until morning). This affects:
> - Menu quantity planning (more courses, more food overall)
> - Late-night/early-morning food service expectations
> - Staff scheduling considerations
> - Multiple meal transitions possible (dinner → late snacks → breakfast)
>
> **Toast Culture**  
> Expect numerous lengthy toasts throughout the evening. Each major guest (parents, grandparents, friends) may give extended speeches. Catering should plan:
> - Toast-specific beverages (vodka, champagne, cognac)
> - Zakuski (закуски) – substantial appetizers served with vodka toasts
> - Glassware quantities higher than Western norms
>
> **Bread and Salt Tradition**  
> Traditional welcoming involves bread and salt (хлеб-соль) when the newlyweds arrive. Consider offering this cultural element or asking if the family wants to provide traditional karavay (round bread).
>
> **Guest Participation Games**  
> Russian receptions frequently include active guest games, stealing of the bride's shoes, and other participatory activities. Ensure catering areas don't interfere with movement-heavy traditions.
>
> **Gift-Giving Expectations**  
> Monetary gifts in envelopes are standard. Some families display gifts or have a designated gift table area that shouldn't conflict with food stations.
>
> **Menu Preferences**  
> Traditional Russian wedding menus often feature:
> - Multiple salads (Olivier, herring under fur coat)
> - Cold cuts and cheese platters
> - Hot dishes (meat, fish, poultry)
> - Multiple dessert options
> - Abundant vodka, wine, and champagne
> - However, modern Russian couples increasingly prefer international/fusion menus
