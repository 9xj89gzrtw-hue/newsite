## Implementation Checklist

### For Each Lead Magnet, Ensure You Have:

□ Compelling headline that communicates value
□ Clear description of what they'll receive
□ Visual representation (mockup, cover image, photo)
□ Benefit-driven bullet points
□ Simple, minimal form fields
□ Clear CTA button with action-oriented copy
□ Privacy/unsubscribe assurance text
□ Thank you page with next steps
□ Welcome/delivery email automation set up
□ Tracking installed (conversion pixel, UTM params)
□ Mobile-optimized display tested

### Promotional Channels to Leverage:

- Website popups (exit-intent, timed, scroll-triggered)
- Blog post CTAs (inline and end-of-post)
- Header/banner announcements
- Social media profiles and posts
- Email signature links
- Business card QR codes
- Venue partner referrals
- Invoice/receipt inserts (for past clients)
