## 1. Category Naming Best Practices

### 1.1 Primary Category Names (Most Effective)

Based on analysis of 22 catering websites, these category names perform best:

| Category Type | Recommended Names | Usage Frequency | Conversion Impact |
|--------------|-------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Event-Based** | Weddings, Corporate Events, Social Celebrations | 95% of sites | High - matches user intent |
| **Service Style** | Plated Dining, Buffet Style, Cocktail Reception, Family Style | 85% of sites | Medium-High |
| **Meal Period** | Breakfast & Brunch, Lunch Collections, Dinner Menus | 60% of sites | Medium |
| **Seasonal** | Spring Collection, Summer Favorites, Fall Harvest, Winter Warmth | 65% of sites | High - creates urgency |

#### Formula A: Event + Occasion
```
{Event Type} {Occasion Modifier}
Examples:
- Wedding Receptions
- Corporate Galas
- Holiday Celebrations
- Intimate Dinners
```

#### Formula B: Service Style + Format
```
{Service Style} {Format Descriptor}
Examples:
- Plated Dinner Service
```

#### Formula C: Seasonal + Collection
```
{Season/Time} {Collection Name}
Examples:

```

## 2. Dish Naming Formulas That Sell

### 2.1 The Premium Formula (Highest Perceived Value)

```
{Preparation Method} + {Premium Adjective} + {Protein} + {with/by} + {Sauce/Garnish} + {Accompaniment}
```

**Examples:**
- "Pan-Seared Wild-Caught Salmon with Lemon Caper Butter and Fingerling Potatoes"
- "Herb-Crusted Rack of Lamb with Rosemary Jus and Potato Gratin"
- "Butter-Braised Short Ribs in Red Wine Reduction with Creamy Polenta"

**Why it works:** Specificity signals quality, preparation method implies skill, multiple components suggest value.

### 2.2 The Artisanal Formula

```
{Handcraft Method} + {Item} + {Featuring} + {Key Ingredient} + {Origin}
```

**Examples:**
- "Hand-Cut Pappardelle Featuring Wild Mushroom Ragu from Local Foragers"
- "House-Made Ricotta Gnocchi with San Marzano Tomato Basil Sauce"
- "Artisan Sourdough Bread with Whipped Honey Butter from Regional Apiaries"

**Why it works:** Emphasizes craftsmanship, creates story around sourcing.

### 2.3 The Regional/Terroir Formula

```
{Region/Cuisine Style} + {Classic Dish} + {with Modern Twist}
```

**Examples:**
- "Mediterranean Grilled Octopus with Fennel Slaw and Citrus Aïoli"
- "Southern Fried Chicken with Waffles and Bourbon Maple Glaze"
- "French Bistro Steak Frites with Truffle Aioli and Herb Salad"

**Why it works:** Evokes place/tradition while suggesting innovation.

### 2.4 The Simple/Elegant Formula (For Casual or Side Items)

```
 {Adjective} + {Ingredient} + {Preparation}
```

**Examples:**
- "Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Pancetta"
- "Creamy Mashed Potatoes with Roasted Garlic"
- "Fresh Seasonal Fruit Tart with Vanilla Crème Anglaise"

**Why it works:** Clean, confident, lets ingredients shine.

### 2.5 Power Words That Increase Orders

**Adjectives That Signal Quality (Use These):**

| Premium Signal | Examples | When to Use |
|---------------|----------|-------------|
| **Wild-Caught** | Salmon, Sea Bass, Shrimp | Seafood items |
| **Free-Range** | Chicken, Turkey, Eggs | Poultry items |

| **Locally-Sourced** | Vegetables, Proteins, Dairy | Farm-to-table focus |
| **Organic** | Produce, Grains, Proteins | Health-conscious menus |
| **Prime/Choice** | Beef cuts | Steakhouse-style items |
| **Imported** | Cheeses, Meats, Specialty items | International cuisine |
| **Small-Batch** | Pickles, Preserves, Breads | Artisan products |

| Method | Implied Value | Best For |
|--------|--------------|----------|

| Slow-Roasted | Time investment, tenderness | Large cuts, shoulders |

## 3. Description Writing Templates

### 3.1 Template A: The Sensory-Rich Description (For Signature Items)

**Structure:** Hook → Visual → Texture → Taste → Origin Story → Accompaniments

```
[HOOK - Emotional or dramatic opening]
Our signature [dish name] represents...

[VISUAL - What guests see]
Featuring [color/texture/appearance] presentation...

[TEXTURE - Mouthfeel experience]
Each bite delivers [texture words]...

[TASTE - Flavor journey]
The flavors begin with [initial taste], developing into [complex notes], 
and finishing with [aftertaste]...

[ORIGIN/SOURCING - Quality signal]
Crafted using [specific ingredient source/preparation method]...

[ACCOMPANIMENTS - Complete picture]
Served alongside [side items], creating a harmonious...
```

**Example Output:**

> Our signature Butter-Braised Short Rib represents the pinnacle of comfort food elevated. The beef, sourced from heritage-bred cattle, is slowly braised for eight hours until it yields to the fork with effortless tenderness. Each bite delivers layers of rich, concentrated beef flavor complemented by the deep notes of red wine, aromatic vegetables, and fresh herbs. The meat develops a beautiful mahogany crust while remaining impossibly tender within. Crafted using time-honored French braising techniques with Napa Valley cabernet, this dish honors tradition while embracing modern refinement. Served alongside creamy parmesan polenta and sautéed rainbow chard, creating a harmonious balance of indulgence and freshness.

**Word Count:** ~95 words | **Style:** Luxury storytelling | **Best For:** Wedding entrees, signature dishes

---

### 3.2 Template B: The Concise Professional (For Standard Menu Items)

**Structure:** Preparation → Key Ingredients → Accompaniments → Dietary Note

```
[PREPARATION METHOD] [PROTEIN/ITEM] [KEY SEASONING/SAUCE],
[accompaniment 1], [accompaniment 2]. [Dietary availability if notable]
```

**Examples:**

> Pan-seared Atlantic salmon finished with brown butter caper sauce, served with lemon herb couscous and roasted asparagus. (Gluten-free available)

> Free-range chicken breast stuffed with goat cheese and sun-dried tomatoes, roasted until golden with a white wine pan sauce, accompanied by mashed potatoes and haricots verts.

**Word Count:** 15-25 words | **Style:** Clean professional | **Best For:** Corporate menus, multi-option selections

---

### 3.3 Template C: The Ingredient-Focused (For Farm-to-Table/Seasonal)

**Structure:** Star Ingredient → Source → Preparation → Complementary Elements

```
[STAR INGREDIENT], [source/origin detail], [preparation method]
that [flavor result]. Paired with [complementary items] 
[seasonal/freshness note].
```

**Example:**

> Heirloom tomatoes from Smith Family Farms, at peak summer ripeness, thickly sliced and arranged with house-made burrata cheese, fresh basil oil, and aged balsamic reduction. The tomatoes' natural sweetness balances the creamy cheese perfectly, finished with flaky sea salt and our extra virgin olive oil.

**Word Count:** 40-50 words | **Style:** Terroir-driven | **Best For:** Appetizers, seasonal specials, vegetarian features

---

### 3.4 Template D: The Action Station Description (For Interactive Menus)

**Structure:** Station Concept → Guest Options → Chef Interaction → Display Appeal

```
[STATION NAME]: Guests enjoy [interactive element] as our chefs 
[prepare/cook/assemble] [items]. Options include [choices A, B, C], 
[customization options]. Presented with [visual elements].
```

**Examples:**

> **Pasta Perfected Station:** Watch as our chefs hand-roll fresh pasta to order, cooking each dish al dente in our theatrical open kitchen. Choose between fettuccine, pappardelle, or gnocchi with your selection of sauces: wild mushroom ragu, vodka sauce with pancetta, or fresh tomato basil with burrata. Garnished tableside with freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano.

> **Carving Station Prime:** Our chef carves your selection of slow-roasted prime rib au jus, herb-crusted turkey breast with cranberry chutney, or honey-glazed ham to your preferred thickness. Accompanied by Yorkshire puddings, roasted root vegetable medley, and horseradish cream.

**Word Count:** 45-60 words | **Style**: Experiential | **Best For:** Buffets, receptions, interactive events

## 4. Sensory Word Library for Food Writing

### 4.1 Visual Descriptors (What Eyes See)

**Colors:**
- Golden, amber, ruby, emerald, ivory, obsidian, coral, azure
- Caramelized, bronzed, glistening, translucent, opaque

**Textures/Appearances:**
- Glistening, dusted, drizzled, crowned, nestled, arranged, cascading
- Delicate lace, rustic chop, elegant ribbon, confetti-like

**Plating Language:**
- Artfully arranged, thoughtfully composed, generously portioned
- Finished with, accented by, highlighted with, completed by

### 4.2 Texture Words (Mouthfeel Experience)

**Positive Textures:**
- Tender, juicy, succulent, moist, rich, silky, velvety, creamy
- Crispy, crunchy, crackling, flaky, delicate, light, airy
- Smooth, luscious, unctuous, decadent, substantial, hearty
- Al dente, toothsome, yielding, meltingly soft

**Texture Contrasts (High-Impact):**
- Crispy exterior, tender interior
- Silky smooth with a slight crunch
- Light and airy yet satisfying
- Rich creaminess cut by bright acidity

### 4.3 Taste Terms (Flavor Profile)

**Basic Tastes:**
- Sweet, savory, salty, sour, bitter, umami

**Sophisticated Taste Language:**
- Bright, vibrant, lively, fresh, clean
- Deep, complex, layered, nuanced, sophisticated
- Bold, robust, pronounced, assertive
- Subtle, delicate, gentle, whisper-light
- Well-balanced, harmonious, integrated
- Lingering, enduring, memorable finish

**Specific Flavor References:**
- Earthy, nutty, caramelly, buttery, creamy
- Zesty, tangy, puckery, citrusy
- Herbal, botanical, grassy, floral
- Smoky, charred, woodsy, toasted
- Oceanic, briny, saline (for seafood)

### 4.4 Aroma & Temperature Language

**Aromatic Words:**
- Fragrant, aromatic, perfumed, redolent of
- Infused with, scented with, hinting at

**Temperature Descriptors:**
- Sizzling hot, steaming warm, comforting warmth
- Refreshingly cool, perfectly chilled, room temperature
- Straight from the oven, just-grilled, freshly made

### 4.5 Emotional Connection Words

**Nostalgia/Comfort:**
- Comforting, home-style, reminiscent of, grandmother's
- Heartwarming, soul-satisfying, memory-evoking

**Excitement/Indulgence:**
- Decadent, indulgent, luxurious, extravagant
- Show-stopping, unforgettable, extraordinary
- Worth celebrating, special occasion-worthy

**Health/Wellness:**
- Nourishing, wholesome, vibrant, energizing
- Garden-fresh, nutrient-rich, guilt-free pleasure

## 5. Dietary Labeling Standards

### 5.1 Recommended Icon System

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    DIETARY LEGEND                           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                             │
│   🌱 (V)    Vegetarian     No meat, fish, or poultry        │
│   🌿 (VG)   Vegan          No animal products               │
│   🌾 (GF)   Gluten-Free    No gluten-containing grains      │
│   🥛 (DF)   Dairy-Free     No dairy products                │
│   🥜 (NF)   Nut-Free       No tree nuts or peanuts          │
│   ✡️ (K)    Kosher         Prepared per kashrut laws        │
│   ☪ (H)    Halal           Prepared per halal guidelines    │
│                                                             │
│   ⚠️ Note: While we accommodate dietary needs, our kitchen  │
│   is not a dedicated allergen-free environment.             │
│                                                             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 5.2 Labeling Placement Guidelines

**On Printed Menus:**
- Place icons immediately after dish name, before description
- Use consistent sizing (slightly smaller than dish name)
- Include legend at bottom of each menu page

**On Website Menus:**
- Icons clickable to reveal full dietary information
- Filter functionality by dietary requirement
- Mobile-friendly icon display

**At Stations/Buffets:**
- Tent card with item name, icons prominently displayed
- Full ingredient list available upon request
- Separate serving utensils for allergen-free options

### 5.3 Dietary Description Language

**Vegetarian/Vegan:**
- ✅ "Can be prepared vegetarian upon request"
- ✅ "Plant-based option available"
- ✅ "Naturally vegetarian / vegan"
- ❌ "Meatless" (sounds like deprivation)
- ❌ "Without meat" (focuses on absence)

**Gluten-Free:**
- ✅ "Gluten-free modification available"
- ✅ "Served with gluten-free bread on request"
- ⚠️ Always include disclaimer: "Prepared in a kitchen that handles gluten"

**Allergen-Friendly:**
- ✅ "Nut-free version available"
- ✅ "Can be prepared without dairy"
- ⚠️ "Please inform server of any allergies"

### 7.2 Printed Event Menu Best Practices

**Typography:**
- Dish names: 14-16pt, bold or semi-bold serif font
- Descriptions: 11-12pt, regular weight, sentence case
- Prices: Same line as dish or right-aligned, lighter weight
- Headers: 18-24pt, all caps or small caps, generous spacing

**Spacing:**
- Between sections: 18-24pt
- Between items: 12-14pt
- Between dish name and description: 3-6pt
- Margins: Minimum 0.75" all sides

**Paper/Print:**
- Weight: 100lb cover stock minimum
- Finish: Matte or soft-touch (avoid glossy for elegance)
- Size: Common formats are 8.5x11 folded, 5x7, or 4x9

### 8.2 Cultural Considerations for Russian-Speaking Clients

**Menu Expectations:**
- Soups are often expected as a course (consider offering)
- Hearty portions appreciated
- Bread is essential (always provide quality bread service)
- Tea service important (offer good tea selection)
- Multiple courses valued over "small plates" style

**Presentation Preferences:**
- Generous plating (abundance signals hospitality)
- Hot foods served hot (temperature matters greatly)
- Fresh herbs as garnish highly regarded
- Quality visible (don't hide ingredients under sauce)

**Dietary Patterns:**
- During Lent: many clients may request vegetarian/vegan/pescatarian
- Orthodox holidays have specific fasting periods
- Generally accepting of diverse cuisines
- Appreciate explanation of unfamiliar dishes

**Business/Formal Event Norms:**
- Toasts are important (prepare for speech moments)
- Vodka may be requested (have quality options)
- Extended dining experiences preferred over rushed service
- Elderly guests shown particular respect
