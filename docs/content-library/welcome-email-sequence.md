# Welcome Email Sequence Outline

**Purpose:** Automated email sequence for new subscribers/leads  
**Based on:** Industry best practices + analysis of catering marketing  
**Customization:** Adapt tone, timing, and offers to your brand

---

## Sequence Overview

```
NEW SUBSCRIBER
     ↓
[Email 1: Immediate Welcome] → Delivers lead magnet + sets expectations
     ↓
[Email 2: Day 2-3] → Value email (tip/resource)
     ↓
[Email 3: Day 4-5] → Introduction to company/story
     ↓
[Email 4: Day 7] → Social proof + soft offer
     ↓
[Email 5: Day 10-14] → Segmentation/interest survey
     ↓
[Email 6: Day 21] → Special offer/incentive
     ↓
[Ongoing: Monthly Newsletter] → Regular value + occasional promotions
```

## Email 2: Value Email - Quick Tip (Day 2-3)

**Subject Line Options:**
- "One thing I wish every event planner knew..."
- "[Number] questions to ask before booking a caterer"
- "The #1 mistake we see event hosts make"
- "Quick tip for your [event type] planning"

**Preheader Text:**
"This simple tip could save you stress (and possibly money)..."

**Body Content:**

```
SUBJECT: The #1 question I wish more clients would ask

Hi [First Name],

[Name] here from [Company Name].

After catering [X]+ events over [X] years, I've noticed 
something:

The clients who are happiest with their experience 
aren't the ones who spent the most money.

They're the ones who asked the RIGHT QUESTIONS upfront.

Today I want to share the one question that makes the 
biggest difference:

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

"WHAT HAPPENS WHEN THINGS DON'T GO AS PLANNED?"

Because here's the truth—even with perfect planning, 
unexpected things happen:

→ Weather changes (hello, outdoor weddings!)
→ Guest count shifts last minute  
→ Vendor delays throw off timing
→ Allergies get mentioned at the worst moment

The best caterers don't just have amazing food.
They have CONTINGENCY PLANS and can think on their feet.

When you're interviewing caterers, ask them:
"What's the most unexpected situation you've handled, 
and how did you deal with it?"

Their answer will tell you everything about whether 
you want them at your event.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MY FAVORITE EXAMPLE:

At a wedding last summer, the power went out during 
cocktail hour. 

Our team immediately shifted to our "no-power protocol":
• Charcuterie boards that shine without heating
• Cold appetizers prepped for exactly this scenario  
• Battery-powered ambient lighting we keep on-hand
• Calm communication with guests and venue

The bride later told us she didn't even realize anything 
was wrong until she heard the story later.

That's the kind of partner you want.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FOOD FOR THOUGHT:

What's your biggest concern about hiring a caterer?

Hit reply and let me know—I'd love to address it in a 
future email (or help ease your mind directly!).

Warmly,

[Name]
[Company Name]

P.S. Downloaded your [lead magnet name] yet? It has 
more tips like this:
[ LINK TO LEAD MAGNET ]
```

**Key Elements:**
- ✓ Provides genuine value (not sales pitch)
- ✓ Positions you as expert/authority
- ✓ Tells a memorable story
- ✓ Encourages reply engagement
- ✓ Soft link back to lead magnet

## Automation Settings Reference

### Timing Recommendations

| Email | Delay After Signup | Best Send Time |
|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| Welcome | Immediate (0 min) | Any time (triggered) |
| Value Tip | 2-3 days | Tue-Thu, 10am-2pm |
| Story/Intro | 4-5 days | Tue-Wed, 10am-11am |
| Social Proof | 7 days | Sun-Mon, 6pm-8pm |
| Survey | 10-14 days | Wed-Thu, 11am-1pm |
| Special Offer | 21 days | Sun, 10am-12pm |
| Monthly Newsletter | 30 days, then monthly | Tue or Thu, 10am |

### List Hygiene Notes

- Remove hard bounces immediately
- Monitor engagement; consider re-engagement campaign for 90-day inactive
- Honor unsubscribes within 24 hours (automated)
- Maintain clean list for deliverability
- Segment based on survey responses and engagement patterns

### Testing Recommendations

- Test subject lines with small segment before full send
- A/B test CTA button copy and color
- Test email length (short vs. long-form)
- Test send times for your specific audience
- Track click-through rates on each link
